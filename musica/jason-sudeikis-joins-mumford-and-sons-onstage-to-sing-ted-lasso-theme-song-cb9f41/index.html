Jason Sudeikis Joins Mumford and Sons Onstage to Sing ‘Ted Lasso’ Theme Song

What's happening

Jason Sudeikis made a surprise appearance onstage at Madison Square Garden during a Mumford & Sons concert, where he joined the band to perform the theme song from his Apple TV+ series Ted Lasso.

Who's involved

Jason Sudeikis is an American actor and screenwriter best known for co-creating and starring in Ted Lasso, which has earned him two Golden Globe Awards and four Primetime Emmy Awards. Mumford & Sons is an English folk rock band formed in Greater London, consisting of Marcus Mumford, Ben Lovett, and Ted Dwane.

Why it matters

The crossover moment highlights the cultural reach of Ted Lasso and underscores the show's ability to draw high-profile collaborations and live performance moments. Such surprise appearances at major venues like Madison Square Garden generate significant fan engagement and media attention, extending the show's visibility beyond the streaming platform.

The story

Jason Sudeikis stepped into an unexpected spotlight during Mumford & Sons' concert at Madison Square Garden, taking the stage as a surprise guest to perform the theme song from his acclaimed Apple TV+ series Ted Lasso. The impromptu performance delighted the crowd and created a memorable intersection between the hit comedy-drama and the English folk rock band.

Sudeikis, who co-created and stars in Ted Lasso as the titular American football coach navigating life in English soccer, has become synonymous with the show since its debut. His work on the series has earned him critical acclaim, including two Golden Globe Awards and four Primetime Emmy Awards, establishing him as one of television's most recognizable contemporary figures. Before his rise as an actor and creator, Sudeikis spent a decade with NBC's Saturday Night Live, where he worked as both a writer and cast member from 2003 to 2013.

Mumford & Sons, the folk rock outfit consisting of Marcus Mumford, Ben Lovett, and Ted Dwane, brought the London-formed band's latest tour stop to the iconic New York venue. According to Rolling Stone, the surprise appearance marked a notable moment in the concert, blending the worlds of television entertainment and live music performance. The band's willingness to invite Sudeikis onstage suggests a shared appreciation between the artists and speaks to the cultural footprint Ted Lasso has maintained since its premiere.

The appearance underscores how major streaming properties like Ted Lasso continue to generate cultural moments that extend beyond the screen. By incorporating elements like the show's theme song into live concert settings, artists can engage with fans in new and unexpected ways. For Sudeikis, the Madison Square Garden performance added another layer to his public visibility, while for Mumford & Sons, the collaboration offered a unique talking point for attendees and those following the tour.
