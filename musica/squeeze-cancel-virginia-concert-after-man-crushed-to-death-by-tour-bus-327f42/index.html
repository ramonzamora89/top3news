Squeeze Cancel Virginia Concert After Man Crushed to Death by Tour Bus

What's happening

English rock band Squeeze has canceled a Virginia concert following a fatal accident in which a man was crushed to death by a tour bus. The band released a statement expressing condolences to those affected by the tragedy.

Who's involved

Squeeze, a prominent English rock band that rose to fame during the new wave era of the late 1970s and achieved major commercial success in both the UK and US with hits like "Tempted" and "Black Coffee in Bed." The incident involved a tour bus, likely part of their touring operation.

Why it matters

The cancellation reflects the serious safety risks inherent in large-scale concert touring operations. The incident raises critical questions about tour bus safety protocols and may impact fan expectations and the band's upcoming tour schedule.

The story

English rock band Squeeze has canceled a scheduled Virginia concert after a man was fatally crushed by a tour bus at the venue. According to Rolling Stone, the band issued a statement in the wake of the tragedy: "Our thoughts remain with everyone affected by this tragic event." The specific circumstances surrounding the fatal accident remain under investigation.

Squeeze, which came to prominence in the United Kingdom during the new wave period of the late 1970s, has maintained an active touring schedule in recent years. The band achieved significant commercial success with UK chart hits including "Cool for Cats," "Up the Junction," and "Labelled with Love," and found American audiences through songs like "Tempted" and "Hourglass," becoming part of the Second British Invasion.

The fatal accident involving the tour bus underscores the inherent risks associated with concert touring operations and large vehicles maneuvering in crowded venue environments. Tour buses are essential infrastructure for bands conducting extensive tours, but incidents such as this highlight potential safety vulnerabilities that affect not only artists and crew but also fans and bystanders near performance venues.

The band's decision to cancel the Virginia show represents a respectful response to the tragedy and allows time for investigation into how the fatal incident occurred. As one of the few bands that maintained consistent recording and touring activity across multiple decades since their initial success, Squeeze's cancellation signals the gravity with which they are treating this loss.
