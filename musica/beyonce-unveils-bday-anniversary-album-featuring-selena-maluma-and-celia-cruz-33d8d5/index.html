Beyoncé Unveils ‘B’Day’ Anniversary Album Featuring Selena, Maluma, and Celia Cruz

What's happening

Beyoncé has released an anniversary edition of her 2006 album 'B'Day' as a 31-song digital collection featuring previously unreleased tracks and new collaborations with artists including Selena, Maluma, and Celia Cruz.

Who's involved

Beyoncé, the Grammy-winning singer and one of the most culturally significant entertainers of the 21st century, is behind the project. The anniversary edition includes guest appearances from Latin music stars Selena, Maluma, and the late Celia Cruz, as well as producer Pharrell.

Why it matters

The release extends the cultural reach of one of Beyoncé's landmark albums and introduces unreleased material to fans nearly two decades after the original dropped. The inclusion of Latin artists signals a continued evolution in Beyoncé's artistic collaborations and appeal across genres and demographics.

The story

Beyoncé has unveiled a comprehensive anniversary edition of her 2006 album 'B'Day,' delivering 31 songs in digital format that blend the original project with previously unreleased material and fresh collaborations. According to Rolling Stone, the expanded edition features several new tracks, including a collaboration with Pharrell alongside appearances from Selena, Maluma, and Celia Cruz.

B'Day, Beyoncé's second studio album, was originally released on September 1, 2006, by Columbia Records and Music World Entertainment. The project was recorded in April 2006 and saw production from an array of industry figures including Darkchild, Nellee Hooper, Ne-Yo, The Neptunes, Stargate, and Swizz Beatz. The original release included guest appearances from Jay-Z, Beyoncé's then-boyfriend, while deluxe and international editions expanded the roster with additional artists. The album established itself as a significant moment in Beyoncé's career, showcasing her range across dance, R&B, and hip-hop influences.

The anniversary edition represents a strategic move to revisit one of the singer's commercially and critically successful projects while introducing unreleased recordings to her fanbase. By pairing the original material with new collaborations, the release bridges nearly two decades of musical evolution. The inclusion of Pharrell, who was among the original producers, alongside contemporary Latin music stars creates a cross-generational and cross-cultural narrative around the project.

The 31-song digital format allows for an expansive presentation that goes well beyond traditional deluxe reissue models. This approach gives longtime fans access to vault material while potentially drawing new listeners to a cornerstone album in Beyoncé's discography. The collaborations with Selena, Maluma, and Celia Cruz—spanning different eras and styles within Latin music—underscore a deliberate curation of the additional content.

As one of the most influential entertainers of the 21st century, Beyoncé's decision to revisit B'Day with this anniversary treatment reinforces the album's place in her artistic legacy while demonstrating her continued relevance and ability to attract high-profile collaborators. The project adds another chapter to an already prolific career marked by artistic reinvention and cultural impact.
