Watch Keanu Reeves’ Dogstar Perform ‘Joy’ on ‘Fallon’

What's happening

Keanu Reeves' rock band Dogstar performed their song 'Joy' on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. The performance showcases material from the band's fourth studio album, All in Now, released earlier this year.

Who's involved

Keanu Reeves is a Canadian actor and musician with a four-decade career in film, known for his leading roles in action movies, public persona, and philanthropic work. Time magazine named him one of the 100 most influential people in the world in 2022. Dogstar is his reunited rock band, which has now released their fourth album.

Why it matters

The performance represents a significant moment for Dogstar's reunion and album cycle, bringing the band's music to a mainstream late-night television audience. It demonstrates Reeves' continued commitment to his music career alongside his acting work, reinforcing his multifaceted creative identity beyond cinema.

The story

Keanu Reeves brought his rock band Dogstar to The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon for a live performance of 'Joy,' one of the tracks from their recently released fourth album, All in Now. The appearance marks a notable milestone for the reunited band as they promote their latest studio work to a broad television audience.

Dogstar has been performing the new material from All in Now, which dropped earlier this year. The band's return has generated renewed interest in Reeves' musical pursuits, a side of his creative life that has run parallel to his acclaimed acting career. According to Rolling Stone, the performance on Fallon provided fans with a chance to see the group in action and connect with their latest songs.

Keanu Charles Reeves has maintained a diverse career spanning four decades, with his name synonymous with blockbuster action films and a reputation for both professional excellence and generous philanthropic efforts. His work in music with Dogstar underscores his commitment to artistic expression beyond the screen. The New York Times ranked him as the fourth-greatest actor of the 21st century in 2020, a recognition that speaks to his impact across entertainment.

The late-night television appearance serves as a promotional vehicle for All in Now during a critical period in the album's lifecycle. For a band that has experienced periods of hiatus before reuniting, securing prominent media slots such as Fallon represents momentum in rebuilding their presence and connecting with both longtime fans and new listeners who may know Reeves primarily through his film work.

Dogstar's trajectory from their formation to this latest reunion demonstrates the enduring appeal of the project and Reeves' dedication to the band despite his demanding acting schedule. The performance of 'Joy' on one of late night's most-watched platforms reflects the band's continued efforts to maintain relevance in the rock music landscape.
