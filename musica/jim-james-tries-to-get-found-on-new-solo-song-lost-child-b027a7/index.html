Jim James Tries to Get Found on New Solo Song ‘Lost Child’

What's happening

Jim James, the primary songwriter and vocalist of My Morning Jacket, has released a new solo song titled 'Lost Child.' The track will appear on his upcoming album, Wowed Out, arriving this Friday.

Who's involved

Jim James (James Edward Olliges Jr.) is an American vocalist, guitarist, and producer best known as the primary songwriter of the rock band My Morning Jacket, formed in Louisville, Kentucky in 1998. My Morning Jacket is an experimental, psychedelic rock band that built a significant following beginning in the 2000s, partly through their acclaimed live performances.

Why it matters

The release signals continued solo activity from James alongside his work with My Morning Jacket, demonstrating his ongoing creative output across multiple projects. The album drop this Friday marks a notable moment for fans of both his solo work and the broader My Morning Jacket universe.

The story

Jim James is stepping into the spotlight with a new solo single as his upcoming album approaches release. 'Lost Child,' according to Rolling Stone, will be featured on Wowed Out, an album set to arrive this Friday. The track represents James's latest foray into solo material, continuing a pattern of artistic expansion beyond his primary role in My Morning Jacket.

James, professionally known as Jim James or Yim Yames, has established himself as a multifaceted musician throughout his career. As the vocalist, guitarist, and primary songwriter of My Morning Jacket—the Louisville-based rock band he has anchored since its formation in 1998—James has helped shape the group's distinctive sound, which blends rock and country influences with experimental and psychedelic elements. The band, rounded out by bassist Tom Blankenship, drummer Patrick Hallahan, guitarist Carl Broemel, and keyboardist Bo Koster, built a devoted following beginning in the 2000s in part due to their reputation for dynamic live performances.

Beyond My Morning Jacket, James has also pursued a parallel solo career, releasing several albums that allow him to explore different creative directions. This solo work has given him space to develop his production skills and songwriting voice independently, complementing rather than competing with his responsibilities in the band. The forthcoming album Wowed Out appears to continue this tradition of independent artistic expression.

The release of 'Lost Child' comes as James prepares to share the full scope of Wowed Out with listeners this week. With the album's imminent arrival, the single serves as a preview of what fans can expect from his latest body of solo work. The track's title suggests themes of searching and displacement, potentially offering insight into the album's broader thematic concerns.
