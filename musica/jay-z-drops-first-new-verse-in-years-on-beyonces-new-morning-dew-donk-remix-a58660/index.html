Jay-Z Drops First New Verse in Years on Beyoncé’s New ‘Morning Dew (Donk)’ Remix

What's happening

Jay-Z has released his first new verse in years, appearing on a remix of Beyoncé's archival single 'Morning Dew (Donk)'. The updated version incorporates samples and elements from songs by Soulja Boy, the Isley Brothers, and Sheck Wes.

Who's involved

Jay-Z (Shawn Corey Carter) is an American rapper, businessman, and record executive ranked by Billboard and Vibe in 2023 as the greatest rapper of all time, with an estimated net worth of $2.8 billion as of 2026. Beyoncé Giselle Knowles-Carter is an American singer, songwriter, and businesswoman widely regarded as one of the most culturally significant figures of the 21st century and one of the greatest entertainers of all time.

Why it matters

The collaboration marks a significant moment for Jay-Z's recording output after an extended absence from new material. The remix demonstrates the continued creative partnership between two of music's most influential artists and their ability to reimagine archival material through contemporary production and sample collage.

The story

Jay-Z has returned to the recording booth with a fresh verse on Beyoncé's remix of 'Morning Dew (Donk)', marking his first new recorded material in years. According to Rolling Stone, the updated version weaves together musical elements from Soulja Boy, the Isley Brothers, and Sheck Wes, creating a layered production that builds on the archival original.

The appearance represents a notable moment in Jay-Z's recent career trajectory. Known for his complex lyricism built on double entendres, wordplay, and braggadocio rooted in East Coast hip-hop, the rapper has maintained a more selective approach to recording in recent years while focusing on his extensive business ventures. His return to collaborate with Beyoncé—a partner he has worked with extensively throughout his career—signals continued creative synergy between two artists who have individually shaped the landscape of popular music.

Beyoncé's 'Morning Dew (Donk)' remix represents her ongoing practice of reimagining material and exploring new sonic directions. The production choice to sample and interpolate music from classic soul acts like the Isley Brothers alongside contemporary artists demonstrates the remix's approach to bridging generational sounds. The inclusion of elements from Soulja Boy and Sheck Wes adds another layer of contemporary hip-hop DNA to the track.

The collaborative effort arrives at a time when both artists maintain their positions as defining cultural figures. Jay-Z's wealth of $2.8 billion as of 2026, according to Forbes, makes him the wealthiest music artist in history, a status built on his music catalog and diversified business empire. Beyoncé, meanwhile, continues to be recognized for her vocal ability, artistic reinventions, and live performances that have cemented her status among the greatest entertainers of all time.

The remix showcases how established artists continue to find creative outlets through reimagined versions of existing material rather than traditional album cycles. By bringing Jay-Z back into the fold for a new verse, the project underscores the enduring collaborative relationship between two of hip-hop and popular music's most influential figures.
