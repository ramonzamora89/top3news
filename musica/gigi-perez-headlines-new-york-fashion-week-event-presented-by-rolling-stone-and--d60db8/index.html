Gigi Perez Headlines New York Fashion Week Event Presented by Rolling Stone and Gap Inc.’s Encore Membership Program

What's happening

Gap Inc.'s Encore membership program is hosting an intimate concert featuring singer-songwriter Gigi Perez at New York Fashion Week on September 14. The exclusive event at Cherry Lane Theatre in Greenwich Village is reserved for Encore members and cardmembers, presented in partnership with Rolling Stone.

Who's involved

Gigi Perez is an American singer-songwriter from New Jersey who rose to prominence through viral TikTok hits and later achieved mainstream success with her song "Sailor Song," which peaked at number 22 on the Billboard Hot 100. Gap Inc., headquartered in San Francisco, is one of the largest apparel retailers in the United States, operating over 3,500 stores across its four primary divisions including Gap, Banana Republic, Old Navy, and Athleta.

Why it matters

The event demonstrates how major retailers are leveraging exclusive experiences to deepen customer loyalty and engagement beyond traditional shopping. By aligning with Fashion Week and a rising music artist, Gap Inc.'s Encore program positions itself as a lifestyle membership destination rather than a transactional loyalty card, while Perez gains exposure to an affluent, curated audience during a high-profile industry moment.

The story

Gap Inc. is elevating its Encore membership program by hosting an exclusive performance by singer-songwriter Gigi Perez during New York Fashion Week on September 14. According to Rolling Stone, select Encore members and cardmembers who RSVP will attend an intimate concert at the historic Cherry Lane Theatre in Greenwich Village, marking a strategic blend of fashion, music, and customer loyalty programming.

Gigi Perez has emerged as one of music's breakout artists following viral success on social media. Born in Hackensack, New Jersey, to Cuban parents and raised in West Palm Beach, Florida, she initially gained attention through TikTok for songs including "Celene" and "Sometimes (Backwood)." After a period signed to Interscope Records, during which she released the 2023 EP "How to Catch a Falling Knife," she departed the label and independently released "Sailor Song." The track became a significant commercial success, peaking at number 22 on the Billboard Hot 100 and topping charts in Ireland, Latvia, and other territories.

The concert represents a deliberate strategy by Gap Inc. to deepen engagement with its most valuable customers. The Encore program, enhanced by the partnership with Rolling Stone, offers members exclusive access to premium experiences beyond traditional retail offerings. By hosting a performance at Fashion Week—one of the industry's most prestigious annual events—Gap Inc. connects its brand to cultural moments and emerging talent, positioning Encore as a lifestyle membership rather than a standard loyalty card.

The venue choice underscores the event's emphasis on intimacy and exclusivity. Cherry Lane Theatre, located in Greenwich Village, carries historical significance in New York's cultural landscape. The setting provides a marked contrast to typical corporate-sponsored fashion events, suggesting Gap Inc. is pursuing a more curated, personal approach to customer experience.

For Perez, the performance offers exposure to an affluent, concentrated audience during a crucial period in her career trajectory. Performing at a Fashion Week event, even as part of a brand partnership, provides additional visibility and credibility as she continues to build on the success of "Sailor Song" and expand her presence beyond social media platforms that initially drove her popularity.
