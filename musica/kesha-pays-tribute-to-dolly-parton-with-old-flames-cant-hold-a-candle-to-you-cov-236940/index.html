Kesha Pays Tribute to Dolly Parton With ‘Old Flames (Can’t Hold a Candle to You)’ Cover

What's happening

Kesha has released a cover of Dolly Parton's 1980 hit 'Old Flames (Can't Hold a Candle to You)' as a tribute to the country music legend. The song carries special significance for Kesha, as it was co-written by her mother, Pebe Sebert.

Who's involved

Kesha is an American singer, rapper, and songwriter who has achieved mainstream success with multiple chart-topping albums including Animal (2010) and Rainbow (2017), along with hit singles like 'Tik Tok' and 'Timber'. Dolly Parton is widely regarded as the 'Queen of Country' and one of the most honored country performers in history, with eleven Grammy Awards, three Emmy Awards, and numerous other accolades to her name.

Why it matters

The cover demonstrates cross-generational respect within the music industry and highlights the behind-the-scenes songwriting contributions of Pebe Sebert, Kesha's mother. It also positions Kesha within the tradition of country music artistry, potentially broadening her artistic reach beyond her earlier pop-focused career.

The story

Kesha has paid homage to one of country music's greatest icons by recording a cover of Dolly Parton's 1980 classic 'Old Flames (Can't Hold a Candle to You).' According to Rolling Stone, the tribute takes on added significance given that the song was co-written by Kesha's mother, Pebe Sebert, creating a deeply personal connection to the material for the artist.

Parton's original recording of the track became one of her signature songs during a career that would ultimately earn her recognition as the 'Queen of Country.' Over her decades in the music industry, Parton established herself as one of the most honored country performers in history, accumulating eleven Grammy Awards, three Emmy Awards, and nominations for two Academy Awards and a Tony Award, among numerous other accolades.

Kesha's decision to cover this particular song represents more than a simple tribute to a fellow performer. The inclusion of her mother as a co-writer links three generations of songwriting and performance talent, underscoring the deep roots of musical creativity within her family. Pebe Sebert's contributions to country music songwriting, though perhaps less publicly recognized than her daughter's pop stardom, established a creative legacy that now extends into Kesha's artistic journey.

For Kesha, the cover also reflects an evolution in her artistic identity. Having achieved chart success with albums like Animal (2010) and Rainbow (2017), along with major hits including 'Tik Tok' and collaborations such as 'Timber' with Pitbull, she has demonstrated her capacity to work across multiple musical genres and styles. The Parton cover adds another dimension to her versatility as a performer.

The tribute marks a moment of artistic intersection where contemporary pop and country music traditions meet, with Kesha honoring both Parton's enduring legacy and her own family's contribution to music history through her mother's songwriting work on this 1980 standard.
