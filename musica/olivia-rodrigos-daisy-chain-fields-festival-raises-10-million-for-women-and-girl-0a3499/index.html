Olivia Rodrigo’s Daisy Chain Fields Festival Raises $10 Million for Women and Girls Nonprofits

What's happening

Olivia Rodrigo has organized the Daisy Chain Fields Festival, which raised $10 million for women and girls nonprofits. The proceeds will be distributed to organizations focused on reproductive health, domestic workers' rights, and related causes.

Who's involved

Olivia Rodrigo is an American singer-songwriter and actress who rose to prominence through her roles in Disney Channel's Bizaardvark and Disney+ series High School Musical: The Musical: The Series. The festival benefits organizations including the National Domestic Workers Alliance, Planned Parenthood (a major reproductive healthcare provider in the U.S.), and the National Institute for Reproductive Health.

Why it matters

The fundraising effort demonstrates how celebrity platforms can direct significant resources to advocacy organizations serving underrepresented communities. The $10 million in donations provides meaningful support to nonprofits working on reproductive rights, labor protections, and healthcare access—issues that affect millions of Americans.

The story

Olivia Rodrigo's Daisy Chain Fields Festival has successfully raised $10 million for women and girls nonprofits, according to Rolling Stone. The festival represents a significant charitable initiative by the singer-songwriter, who has leveraged her platform to support causes centered on reproductive health, labor rights, and gender equity.

The beneficiary organizations span multiple areas of advocacy and service. Planned Parenthood, a major nonprofit that provides reproductive and sexual healthcare and education across the United States and globally, will receive a portion of the proceeds. The National Domestic Workers Alliance, which advocates for workers in household service roles, and the National Institute for Reproductive Health are also among the organizations supported by the festival fundraising.

Rodrigo's involvement in organizing the festival reflects her role as both entertainer and activist. The artist, who built her career initially as a child actress and later achieved major success as a recording artist, has used her prominence to amplify causes aligned with her values. Bringing together a music festival format to raise funds for nonprofits represents a strategic approach to mobilizing resources for organizations that often operate with limited budgets.

The scale of the fundraising—$10 million—signals substantial donor engagement and public support for the causes represented by the beneficiary organizations. This level of funding can directly expand services, support advocacy work, and strengthen organizational capacity for groups working in reproductive health, domestic worker protections, and related areas.

The Daisy Chain Fields Festival demonstrates how celebrity-backed events can translate public enthusiasm into concrete philanthropic impact, particularly for causes centered on underrepresented communities and systemic issues. With the funds distributed among multiple organizations, the festival's legacy will extend across several key areas of women's and girls' advocacy and service provision.
