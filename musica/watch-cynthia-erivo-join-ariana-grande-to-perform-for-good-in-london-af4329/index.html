Watch Cynthia Erivo Join Ariana Grande to Perform ‘For Good’ in London

What's happening

Cynthia Erivo joined Ariana Grande on stage at London's O2 Arena to perform 'For Good,' the emotional duet from Wicked. The co-stars also delivered a medley of 'Get Happy' and 'Happy Days Are Here Again' during the show.

Who's involved

Cynthia Erivo and Ariana Grande are the stars of the upcoming Wicked film adaptation, where they play Elphaba and Glinda respectively. Grande is a globally influential pop artist known for her distinctive four-octave vocal range, while Erivo is an accomplished singer and actress bringing the Wicked Witch of the West to life on screen.

Why it matters

The performance marks a rare live collaboration between the two leads ahead of the Wicked film's release, offering fans a preview of their chemistry in the movie. Such high-profile musical moments generate significant buzz for the film and demonstrate the caliber of talent involved in bringing Stephen Schwartz's beloved musical to cinema.

The story

Cynthia Erivo made a surprise appearance at Ariana Grande's London show at the O2 Arena, where the two Wicked co-stars performed together for fans. According to Rolling Stone, the pair sang 'For Good,' the poignant duet from the Stephen Schwartz musical, along with a medley of 'Get Happy' and 'Happy Days Are Here Again.'

Erivo and Grande are set to star in the highly anticipated Wicked film adaptation, with Grande taking on the role of Glinda Upland and Erivo portraying Elphaba Thropp, the future Wicked Witch of the West. The musical itself, with music and lyrics by Schwartz and a book by Winnie Holzman, is loosely based on Gregory Maguire's 1995 novel of the same name. That novel drew inspiration from L. Frank Baum's 1900 novel The Wonderful Wizard of Oz and its 1939 film adaptation, reimagining the story from the perspective of the witches at the center of Dorothy Gale's Kansas adventure.

The London performance provides a tantalizing glimpse of the chemistry between the two leads as they prepare to bring their characters' complex relationship to the screen. 'For Good' is one of the musical's most beloved songs, a tender moment that explores the transformative friendship between Elphaba and Glinda. The live rendition at the O2 allowed Grande's fans in attendance to experience the song as performed by both actresses who will carry it in the film.

Grande, who is recognized as one of the most influential artists in contemporary music with her distinctive whistle-register vocal range, has built a career that extends across singing, songwriting, and acting. The appearance alongside Erivo underscores the star power behind the Wicked film project and demonstrates the commitment to musical authenticity in adapting Schwartz's work for cinema.

The surprise duet generated excitement among fans and industry observers tracking the progression toward the film's release. Such live collaborations between major film co-stars remain relatively uncommon, making the O2 performance a memorable moment for audiences present and those following the story online.
