See Charli XCX Join the Strokes for ‘Take It or Leave It’ at Outside Lands

What's happening

Charli XCX made a surprise appearance at Outside Lands music festival in San Francisco on Saturday night, joining The Strokes on stage during their set to perform 'Take It or Leave It.' The appearance comes just days after the two artists competed in a high-profile album sales battle that Charli won decisively.

Who's involved

Charli XCX is a British singer and songwriter who rose to prominence after co-writing and featuring on Icona Pop's 2012 hit 'I Love It'; The Strokes are an American rock band formed in New York City in 1998, known as leaders of the early-2000s garage rock and post-punk revival movements, fronted by Julian Casablancas.

Why it matters

The collaboration highlights a moment of cultural goodwill between two artists who were recently positioned as commercial competitors, suggesting camaraderie beneath the surface-level sales narrative. The surprise performance at a major festival spotlights both artists to a broad audience and demonstrates The Strokes' continued relevance and willingness to collaborate across genre lines.

The story

Charli XCX took the stage at San Francisco's Outside Lands music festival on Saturday night as a surprise guest during The Strokes' headlining set, performing 'Take It or Leave It' with the legendary rock band. The appearance marks a notable moment of collaboration between two artists who had just competed in a widely publicized album sales race.

The Strokes, an American rock band formed in New York City in 1998, were instrumental in the early-2000s garage rock revival and post-punk revival movements. Led by vocalist and primary songwriter Julian Casablancas, the band—which includes guitarists Nick Valensi and Albert Hammond Jr., bassist Nikolai Fraiture, and drummer Fabrizio Moretti—remains a significant force in rock music. The group's Saturday headlining slot at Outside Lands positioned them as one of the festival's marquee acts.

Charli XCX, the British singer and songwriter born Charlotte Emma Aitchison, has built her career as a genre-bending pop innovator. After posting songs on Myspace in 2008 and entering the London rave scene, she signed with Asylum Records in 2010. Her breakthrough came in 2012 when she co-wrote and was featured on Icona Pop's 'I Love It,' which became her first number-one UK hit and achieved global success, according to Rolling Stone.

Julian Casablancas acknowledged the recent chart competition between the two artists with characteristic humor. 'She won. She won by a lot,' he quipped, referring to their competing album releases. Rather than creating lasting tension, the sales battle appears to have fostered a mutual respect that manifested in the surprise collaboration. The choice to bring Charli XCX on stage during their set underscores The Strokes' openness to cross-genre partnerships and contemporary pop sensibilities.

The performance at Outside Lands, one of San Francisco's premier music festivals, gave both artists an opportunity to showcase a different dynamic—blending The Strokes' rock credentials with Charli XCX's contemporary pop approach. For festival attendees, the surprise guest appearance delivered an unexpected highlight that transcended the typical album-cycle competition narrative dominating music industry headlines in recent weeks.
