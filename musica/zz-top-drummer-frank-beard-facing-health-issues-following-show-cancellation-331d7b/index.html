ZZ Top Drummer Frank Beard Facing Health Issues Following Show Cancellation

What's happening

ZZ Top drummer Frank Beard is dealing with health issues that have forced the cancellation of at least one show. Billy Gibbons and bassist Elwood Francis will continue the tour with a replacement drummer.

Who's involved

Frank Beard is the longtime drummer for ZZ Top, the legendary Houston rock band formed in 1969. Billy Gibbons, the band's vocalist and guitarist, leads the effort to keep the tour moving forward with a fill-in musician.

Why it matters

The cancellation marks a significant disruption for one of rock's most enduring acts, which has maintained a touring presence for five decades. The health setback raises questions about the band's future stability as it navigates life after the 2021 death of bassist Dusty Hill.

The story

ZZ Top drummer Frank Beard is facing undisclosed health issues that have led to the cancellation of at least one show, according to Rolling Stone. Billy Gibbons and bassist Elwood Francis intend to press forward with the tour, employing a fill-in drummer to complete scheduled dates.

Frank Beard has been a core member of ZZ Top since the band's formation in Houston, Texas, in 1969. Alongside Gibbons and bassist Dusty Hill, Beard formed the rhythm section that helped define the band's signature blues-based rock sound. The trio became as recognizable for their visual identity—Gibbons and Hill famously performed in matching sunglasses, hats, and long beards—as for their sly, humorous lyrics and commanding live presence.

The band has built a five-decade legacy on the foundation of relentless touring and a reputation for explosive live performances. ZZ Top's ability to connect with audiences on stage has remained a cornerstone of their identity even as the music industry transformed around them. The band's resilience has been tested before: Dusty Hill's death in 2021 forced ZZ Top to pause operations and reconsider its future.

With Beard now sidelined due to health concerns, the decision to continue with a substitute drummer represents an effort to honor existing tour commitments while addressing the immediate challenge. Elwood Francis, who took over bass duties following Hill's passing, will remain with the band. Rolling Stone reports that a spokesperson confirmed the touring arrangement, though specific details about Beard's condition have not been disclosed.

The cancellation comes at a moment when the rock world continues to process the loss of several iconic musicians. ZZ Top's willingness to adapt and move forward suggests the band's commitment to maintaining its presence, even as its original lineup continues to contract.
