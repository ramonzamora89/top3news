Olivia Rodrigo and Yungblud Join Smashing Pumpkins for Nostalgia-Fueled Lollapalooza Set

What's happening

Smashing Pumpkins frontman Billy Corgan performed a nostalgia-driven set at Lollapalooza featuring surprise guest appearances by Olivia Rodrigo and Yungblud. The band delivered deep cuts and fan favorites including "Luna" and "Thirty-Three" during the Chicago festival performance.

Who's involved

Smashing Pumpkins is an American alternative rock band formed in Chicago in 1988, led by Billy Corgan as primary songwriter and sole constant member since inception. The current lineup includes Corgan, guitarist James Iha, and drummer Jimmy Chamberlin. Pop-rock artist Olivia Rodrigo and alternative artist Yungblud joined as special guests.

Why it matters

The collaboration signals continued cross-generational appeal for legacy rock acts, with newer artists like Rodrigo and Yungblud lending contemporary relevance to Smashing Pumpkins' catalog. The performance showcases Lollapalooza's role as a cultural touchstone for both established and emerging artists in alternative music.

The story

The Smashing Pumpkins delivered a career-spanning set at Lollapalooza, the annual four-day music festival held in Chicago's Grant Park, with Billy Corgan enlisting surprise guest appearances from Olivia Rodrigo and Yungblud. According to Rolling Stone, the band performed "Luna" and "Thirty-Three," tapping into the nostalgia that has fueled the group's resurgence since reuniting in 2006.

Lollapalooza, which settled permanently in Chicago in 2005 after beginning as a touring event in 1991, attracts an estimated 400,000 attendees each July and has become one of the largest music festivals in America. The event spans multiple genres including alternative rock, heavy metal, punk rock, hip hop, and electronic dance music, making it a significant platform for both legacy acts and emerging talent.

Corgan, who founded Smashing Pumpkins in Chicago in 1988 with guitarist James Iha, bassist D'arcy Wretzky, and drummer Jimmy Chamberlin, has remained the band's creative anchor through numerous lineup changes. The current iteration features Corgan, Iha, and Chamberlin, maintaining the core of the group that became known for its densely layered, genre-blending alternative rock sound.

The inclusion of Rodrigo and Yungblud as surprise guests reflects the broader reach of Smashing Pumpkins' influence across generations of alternative and pop-rock artists. Rodrigo's chart dominance in recent years and Yungblud's presence in alternative circles underscore how classic '90s rock continues to resonate with contemporary audiences and performers.

The Lollapalooza performance marks another chapter in Smashing Pumpkins' sustained touring presence and cultural relevance. By showcasing both deep cuts like "Luna" and the more accessible "Thirty-Three," Corgan's set balanced the band's catalog depth with songs that have endured in popular memory.
