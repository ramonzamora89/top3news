Watch Noah Kahan and Surprise Guest Chappell Roan Perform ‘California’ at Rose Bowl Stadium

What's happening

Noah Kahan performed 27 songs at his sold-out The Great Divide tour stop at Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, California on Saturday, with surprise guest Chappell Roan joining him on stage. The two artists performed 'California' together during the concert.

Who's involved

Noah Kahan is an American singer-songwriter whose 2022 album Stick Season became a mainstream commercial breakthrough, earning him a 2023 Grammy nomination for Best New Artist. Chappell Roan (Kayleigh Rose Amstutz) is known for her camp and drag queen–influenced style and has become an emerging voice in contemporary pop music.

Why it matters

The collaboration highlights the growing prominence of both artists within the music industry, with Kahan continuing to build momentum from his recent Grammy recognition and Roan gaining visibility through high-profile appearances. Surprise guest appearances at major venue concerts generate significant fan engagement and social media buzz, amplifying reach beyond the ticketed audience.

The story

Noah Kahan brought his The Great Divide tour to the Rose Bowl Stadium in Pasadena, California on Saturday, delivering a 27-song setlist to a sold-out crowd. The performance marked a significant moment when surprise guest Chappell Roan joined Kahan on stage to perform 'California' together, according to Rolling Stone.

Kahan's rise in the music industry has been steady and significant. His breakthrough single, 'Hurt Somebody', achieved gold status in the United States and charted across multiple international markets. That success was followed by his debut album Busyhead in 2019, establishing him as a singer-songwriter worth watching. Over the next five years, he released two more albums: I Was / I Am in 2021 and Stick Season in 2022, with the latter becoming his mainstream commercial breakthrough and leading to his Grammy nomination for Best New Artist in 2023.

Chappell Roan's appearance as a surprise guest underscores her growing prominence in popular music. Known for her distinctive camp and drag queen–influenced aesthetic, Roan represents a different artistic sensibility than Kahan, making the collaboration a notable cross-genre moment. The duet on 'California' suggests a creative connection between the two artists and likely delighted fans who were not expecting the performance.

The Rose Bowl Stadium performance represents a major milestone for Kahan's touring career, with the venue's capacity and sold-out status indicating the scale of his current popularity. A 27-song setlist suggests an extensive career retrospective, allowing fans to experience both breakthrough hits and deeper album cuts. Surprise guest appearances at major venue concerts have become a notable draw for fans, with social media amplifying such moments far beyond the live audience.

The Great Divide tour continues to build on the momentum Kahan established with Stick Season, bringing his music to increasingly larger venues across the country. These high-profile performances cement his position as more than a streaming phenomenon, establishing him as a legitimate touring artist capable of selling out major stadiums.
