Radiohead Announce Australia and Japan Shows

What's happening

Radiohead has announced a series of concert dates across Australia and Japan scheduled for 2027. The English rock band will play a combined total of 21 shows across both regions.

Who's involved

Radiohead is an English rock band formed in Abingdon, Oxfordshire, in 1985, featuring Thom Yorke on vocals, brothers Jonny Greenwood and Colin Greenwood on guitar and bass respectively, Ed O'Brien on guitar, and Philip Selway on drums. The band has been a major force in alternative rock since the mid-1990s, known for their experimental approach to music.

Why it matters

The announcement signals Radiohead's continued touring activity and gives fans in Asia-Pacific regions concrete dates to plan around. For the band, these 2027 dates represent a significant commitment to live performance, maintaining their presence as one of rock's most influential acts nearly four decades after their formation.

The story

Radiohead has announced plans to bring their tour to Australia and Japan in 2027, with the band set to perform a combined 21 dates across both territories, according to Pitchfork. The announcement represents a significant addition to what is shaping up to be an active year for the influential English rock group.

The band, which formed in Abingdon, Oxfordshire, in 1985, has established itself as one of alternative rock's most innovative and enduring acts. Fronted by Thom Yorke and featuring brothers Jonny Greenwood and Colin Greenwood on guitar and bass, alongside Ed O'Brien on guitar and Philip Selway on drums, Radiohead's experimental approach has continually pushed the boundaries of the genre since their breakthrough in the mid-1990s.

The 2027 Australia and Japan shows continue Radiohead's pattern of maintaining an active touring schedule. These dates will allow fans in the Asia-Pacific region to experience the band live, following performances the group has undertaken in other territories. The announcement of 21 specific dates indicates a substantial commitment to both markets, suggesting the band is treating these regions as priority destinations for their touring activities.

Radiohead's touring activity remains closely followed by their international fan base, given the band's reputation for both studio innovation and compelling live performances. The specifics of the venue sizes and ticket availability for the 2027 dates are expected to be announced in further detail as the touring schedule is finalized.
