Watch Chance the Rapper Revisit Reflective Track ‘Same Drugs’ on ‘Fallon’

What's happening

Chance the Rapper performed 'Same Drugs,' a reflective track from his acclaimed mixtape Coloring Book, during an appearance on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon. The performance marks a celebration of the project's 10th anniversary.

Who's involved

Chance the Rapper (born Chancelor Johnathan Bennett) is a Chicago-based rapper who rose to prominence with his 2013 mixtape Acid Rap and has established himself as a leading independent artist in hip-hop. The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon is NBC's late-night talk show where musical guests regularly perform.

Why it matters

The performance underscores the lasting cultural impact of Coloring Book, a project that defined a decade of independent hip-hop releases. For Chance, revisiting seminal work on a major platform keeps the milestone in public conversation and demonstrates the track's continued relevance to both the artist and his fanbase.

The story

Chance the Rapper delivered a performance of 'Same Drugs' on The Tonight Show Starring Jimmy Fallon, marking a nostalgic moment as the artist celebrates a decade since the release of his influential mixtape Coloring Book. According to Rolling Stone, the appearance highlights the reflective nature of the track and its place within the broader context of the project's legacy.

'Same Drugs' stands out as one of the more introspective cuts from Coloring Book, showcasing the emotional depth that helped define the mixtape. The track exemplifies Chance's ability to blend vulnerability with his characteristic production sensibilities, themes that resonated with listeners upon the project's initial release and continue to resonate today.

Chance the Rapper's path to this milestone began with his breakthrough success on Acid Rap in 2013, which established him as a central figure in Chicago hip-hop and among the wave of independent artists reshaping the commercial landscape. That early momentum set the stage for Coloring Book, which would further cement his status and demonstrate his evolution as an artist capable of both commercial appeal and artistic substance.

The Fallon appearance comes as artists increasingly revisit milestone projects during anniversary windows, using late-night television platforms to reconnect with existing audiences and introduce seminal work to newer listeners. For Chance, the performance serves as both a celebration of past achievement and a reminder of the artistic vision that defined a crucial moment in his career and in hip-hop more broadly.

Coloring Book's enduring influence reflects the broader significance of mixtape culture in establishing independent artists outside traditional label structures. By choosing to perform 'Same Drugs'—a track that emphasizes introspection over flash—Chance reinforces the project's identity as thoughtful and substantive, qualities that have contributed to its staying power in conversations about contemporary hip-hop.
