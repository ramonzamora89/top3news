Olivia Rodrigo Shines With Stevie Nicks, Sarah McLachlan, Alanis Morissette at Daisy Chain

What's happening

Olivia Rodrigo headlined her inaugural all-women music festival, Daisy Chain, bringing together legendary artists including Stevie Nicks, Sarah McLachlan, and Alanis Morissette for a collaborative performance.

Who's involved

Olivia Rodrigo, the rising pop star, created the festival and performed alongside industry icons Stevie Nicks (legendary Fleetwood Mac singer-songwriter), Alanis Morissette (Canadian-American alt-rock icon with seven Grammy Awards and 60+ million records sold), and Sarah McLachlan. The event represents a convergence of generations in women-led music.

Why it matters

The festival demonstrates Rodrigo's influence and ability to convene established artists at a career-defining moment, while celebrating women in music across multiple generations. It signals the continued cultural relevance of female-fronted rock and alternative music, and positions Rodrigo as both a performer and curator shaping the industry's future direction.

The story

Olivia Rodrigo capped her inaugural all-women music festival, Daisy Chain, with a stunning headlining performance that brought together three generations of influential female artists. The event showcased Rodrigo sharing the stage with her musical heroes, creating a rare moment of intergenerational collaboration that highlighted the depth and legacy of women in rock and alternative music.

The festival's headlining set featured performances from Stevie Nicks, the legendary singer-songwriter known for her decades-long career with Fleetwood Mac and as a solo artist, alongside Canadian-American alt-rock pioneer Alanis Morissette. Morissette, who became a cultural phenomenon in the 1990s and gained international fame for her emotive mezzo-soprano voice and confessional songwriting, has sold more than 60 million records worldwide and earned seven Grammy Awards throughout her storied career. Sarah McLachlan rounded out the lineup, completing what Rolling Stone reports was a triumphant inaugural event.

Rodrigo's decision to create an all-women festival and invite these established artists to perform alongside her speaks to her influence and stature in the music industry despite her relative youth as a recording artist. The lineup itself represents a carefully curated celebration of female artistry, drawing connections between Nicks' pioneering work in rock music, Morissette's alt-rock dominance of the 1990s, and Rodrigo's contemporary pop-rock sound. By positioning her heroes on stage with her rather than simply performing solo, Rodrigo created a moment that honored the artists who shaped her musical foundation.

The success of Daisy Chain's inaugural year signals both the appetite for women-centered music festivals and Rodrigo's emerging role as not just a performer but a curator and cultural influencer. Her choice to spotlight established legends alongside emerging artists sent a clear message about the importance of acknowledging the legacy of women in rock while simultaneously building momentum for the next generation. The performance demonstrated how contemporary artists can leverage their platform to celebrate those who came before them while creating spaces explicitly designed around female voices and perspectives.

For attendees and observers, the festival represented a rare convergence of iconic performers across different eras, each bringing distinct approaches to songwriting, performance, and artistic expression. Rodrigo's headlining set became more than just a concert moment—it functioned as a statement about artistic lineage, mutual respect among female musicians, and the enduring power of rock and alternative music made by women.
