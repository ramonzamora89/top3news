See Kacey Musgrave Cover Dolly Parton’s ‘Here You Come Again’ in Boston

What's happening

Kacey Musgraves performed a cover of Dolly Parton's 'Here You Come Again' during a Boston concert. The country singer also dedicated her song 'Rainbow' to Parton, disclosing that the late icon inspired the track.

Who's involved

Kacey Musgraves is an American singer-songwriter who broke through with her 2012 hit 'Merry Go 'Round' after signing with Mercury Nashville, following early appearances on Nashville Star. Dolly Parton, the legendary 'Queen of Country,' was one of the most successful country music performers in history with over 100 million records sold and a major cultural legacy spanning music, film, and philanthropy.

Why it matters

The performance represents a direct artistic tribute to Parton following her recent death, highlighting her enduring influence on contemporary country musicians like Musgraves. By revealing that Parton inspired 'Rainbow,' Musgraves demonstrates how foundational Parton's work remains to younger generations of songwriters, cementing her legacy beyond her lifetime.

The story

Kacey Musgraves paid tribute to the late Dolly Parton during a recent performance in Boston, taking the stage to cover Parton's classic 'Here You Come Again.' The gesture came as part of a broader moment of remembrance for the country music icon, as Musgraves also dedicated her song 'Rainbow' to Parton's memory, according to Rolling Stone, revealing that the legendary artist had been a creative inspiration behind the track.

Parton, who passed away recently, left behind a remarkable legacy as one of the most successful country music performers in history. Dubbed the 'Queen of Country,' she amassed more than 100 million records sold throughout her career, placing her among the best-selling music artists of all time. Beyond her musical achievements, Parton became known worldwide for her cultural influence and extensive philanthropic work, including the founding of Dollywood and the Dollywood Foundation, which have benefited countless communities.

Musgraves herself has carved out a significant career in country music since breaking through in the early 2010s. After appearing on the USA Network's Nashville Star competition in 2007, she went on to sign with Mercury Nashville in 2012 and released the hit single 'Merry Go 'Round,' which established her as a major force in contemporary country music. Her willingness to honor Parton's legacy publicly underscores the deep influence the veteran artist maintained across generations of performers.

The Boston performance represents one of many tributes that have emerged following Parton's death, as artists across the country music community have reflected on her impact and contributions to the genre. By choosing to cover 'Here You Come Again'—one of Parton's signature songs—and dedicating original material to her memory, Musgraves joined a chorus of peers acknowledging the void left by Parton's passing and the immeasurable mark she left on country music and American culture.
