15 Wonderful Restaurants in Key West

What's happening

Eater Miami has compiled a guide to 15 exceptional restaurants across Key West, highlighting the island's thriving culinary scene. The feature showcases the destination's welcoming culture and its abundant fresh seafood offerings prepared in diverse styles.

Who's involved

Eater Miami, the South Florida edition of the food-focused publication Eater, has curated this restaurant roundup. Key West itself, the southernmost city in Florida located at the end of the Florida Keys archipelago, serves as the dining destination being spotlighted.

Why it matters

This guide reinforces Key West's reputation as a premier food destination and tourism draw, emphasizing how culinary experiences contribute to the island's appeal as an escape destination. For both residents and visitors, access to restaurant recommendations helps support the local hospitality economy and encourages exploration of the island's diverse dining options.

The story

Key West continues to establish itself as a culinary hotspot, with Eater Miami highlighting 15 standout restaurants that exemplify the island's food culture. The coral cay island, which sits at the southernmost tip of Florida and comprises multiple islands including Dredgers Key, Fleming Key, and Sunset Key, has developed a restaurant scene as distinctive as its geography.

The island's appeal as a dining destination stems partly from its identity as one of the country's most welcoming destinations, attracting visitors and residents seeking an escape from everyday life. According to Eater Miami, Key West's food scene does not disappoint those seeking quality dining experiences, with fresh seafood at the forefront of the local culinary identity.

Freshly caught seafood dominates menus across Key West's dining establishments, available in preparations ranging from grilled to fried. The guide references offerings such as baskets of peel-and-eat shrimp and other preparations that showcase the island's access to quality local catches. This emphasis on seafood reflects both the island's geographic location in the Straits of Florida and the cultural traditions that have developed around marine resources.

The curation of these 15 restaurants serves restaurant-goers planning a visit to Key West by identifying establishments worth seeking out. The variety implied in such a list suggests diverse price points and dining styles, from casual to refined, accommodating different preferences and budgets among the island's visitors and residents.

Key West's positioning as a food destination complements its broader reputation for hospitality and cultural openness, creating an integrated appeal that draws people to the southernmost city in the United States for more than just its beaches and historic charm.
