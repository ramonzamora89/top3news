Miami Spice Back with New Reserve Tier and 300 Restaurants

What's happening

Miami Spice Restaurant Month, now in its 25th year, is returning for two months starting August 1, 2026, with an expanded roster of over 300 participating restaurants offering three-course prix-fixe menus at minimum 30% discounts. The program introduces a new reserve tier this year, adding another layer of dining options for the region's food enthusiasts.

Who's involved

The Greater Miami Convention & Visitors Bureau (GMCVB) oversees and promotes Miami Spice, an annual initiative designed to showcase the region's diverse culinary scene. The program engages more than 300 restaurants across Miami-Dade County, ranging from casual establishments to fine dining venues.

Why it matters

Miami Spice drives tourism and restaurant revenue during the slower summer months by offering significant savings to diners while increasing foot traffic for participating establishments. The expansion to 300+ restaurants and introduction of a reserve tier signals growing confidence in Miami's dining market and the program's ability to attract both locals and visitors seeking quality dining experiences at accessible price points.

The story

Miami Spice Restaurant Month is making its return this August through September, marking the 25th edition of the popular dining promotion that has become a fixture on the region's culinary calendar. Running from August 1 through September 30, 2026, the program offers diners the chance to experience three-course prix-fixe menus with savings of at least 30% at participating venues. According to Miami Curated, this year's iteration features more than 300 restaurants, expanding the already robust roster of previous years.

The Greater Miami Convention & Visitors Bureau (GMCVB), which administers the program, has designed Miami Spice to energize the dining scene during the summer season, when tourism typically declines due to heat and humidity. By offering substantial discounts on full meals at quality restaurants, the program attracts both locals looking to explore new venues and visitors planning trips around the savings opportunity. The prix-fixe format encourages diners to sample restaurants they might otherwise hesitate to visit, while guaranteed covers help establishments manage cash flow during a slower period.

A notable addition this year is the introduction of a reserve tier, which provides a new category within the program's structure. While specific details about the reserve tier's pricing, availability, or participating restaurants remain limited, this expansion suggests GMCVB is responding to demand for varied dining experiences at different price points and quality levels. The move acknowledges that Miami's restaurant landscape has matured considerably since the program's inception 25 years ago, with an increasingly diverse range of establishments from emerging neighborhood spots to established culinary destinations.

The expansion to over 300 participating restaurants underscores Miami's position as a major dining destination. The variety of cuisines, price points, and neighborhood locations means diners have genuine choice in where to spend their Miami Spice vouchers. From Latin-inspired fare reflecting the region's cultural heritage to contemporary American and international menus, the participating roster mirrors the city's culinary diversity.

For the participating restaurants, Miami Spice represents a proven strategy for building customer loyalty and generating volume during months when demand might otherwise soften. The guaranteed discount structure is transparent to diners and manageable for restaurants, allowing both parties to benefit from the arrangement. Many establishments report that participants in prior years return for future transactions, converting a promotional diner into a regular customer.
