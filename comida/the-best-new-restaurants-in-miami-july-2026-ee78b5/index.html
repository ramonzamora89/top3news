The Best New Restaurants in Miami, July 2026

What's happening

Eater Miami has released a curated list of 24 outstanding new restaurants currently operating in the city. The roundup aims to answer the frequent question from locals and visitors about where to eat right now in Miami's dynamic dining scene.

Who's involved

Eater Miami, the local dining publication under the Vox Media umbrella, compiles recommendations from its network of tipsters, readers, friends, and family members embedded in the Miami food community. The publication is known for its Essential 38 list and Heatmap, which serve as comprehensive guides to the city's restaurant landscape.

Why it matters

As Miami's restaurant scene continues to evolve with new openings, having a curated list helps diners navigate options and supports emerging establishments by directing traffic. The publication's methodology—drawing from community input rather than critics alone—reflects how modern food media engages readers as active participants in shaping dining recommendations.

The story

Miami's restaurant landscape is experiencing another wave of new openings, and Eater Miami has taken stock of 24 establishments that deserve attention right now. The publication regularly fields the same question from its audience: where should I eat in Miami today? To answer that question, the team has gone beyond its established guides to highlight fresh venues that are making an impression on the local dining community.

The list emerges from Eater Miami's broad network of contributors and insiders. Rather than relying solely on critic reviews, the publication draws recommendations from local tipsters, readers, friends, and family members who eat regularly across the city. This crowdsourced approach captures a wider range of perspectives and tastes than traditional restaurant criticism alone.

For readers who have already worked through Eater Miami's Miami Essential 38—the publication's longer-standing definitive list—this new roundup offers a natural next step. The Essential 38 provides a foundational guide to the city's most important restaurants across various cuisines and styles. Alongside that list, Eater Miami maintains the Miami Heatmap, another tool for diners seeking orientation in the city's complex food scene.

According to Eater Miami, the timing of this roundup reflects the pace at which new restaurants are opening and closing in the city. The 24 venues highlighted represent the kind of establishments that are capturing attention among local diners right now, though the publication notes that this list will inevitably shift as the restaurant landscape continues to change. Some favorites may not appear here, a reflection of the difficult task of capturing a moving target.

The curation process speaks to a broader shift in how dining guides operate in the digital age. Rather than presenting a static, annually updated book, publications can now offer dynamic, frequently refreshed recommendations that respond to real-time changes in the restaurant world. This allows both diners and restaurants to stay current with what's actually happening on the ground.

For anyone planning to explore Miami's dining scene this summer and beyond, the list serves as a starting point for discovering where chefs and restaurateurs are pushing the local food culture forward.
