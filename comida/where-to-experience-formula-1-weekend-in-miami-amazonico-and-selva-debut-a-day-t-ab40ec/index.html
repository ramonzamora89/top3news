Where to Experience Formula 1 Weekend in Miami: Amazónico and Selva Debut a Day-to-Night Race Destination

What's happening

Two Miami restaurants, Amazónico and Selva, are launching an integrated day-to-night Formula 1 experience during Miami Grand Prix weekend, offering race day brunch, rooftop gatherings, and Grand Prix viewing parties in the Brickell neighborhood.

Who's involved

Amazónico and Selva are upscale dining establishments in Miami's Brickell district that are collaborating to create a unified F1 weekend hospitality experience. The Miami Grand Prix itself is held annually at the Miami International Autodrome, located near Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens.

Why it matters

This initiative reflects how major sporting events like Formula 1 are expanding beyond the track to create broader lifestyle and hospitality experiences in host cities. For Miami's restaurant and tourism sectors, it positions the city's dining venues as central to the Grand Prix experience and drives weekend foot traffic and revenue during the high-profile racing event.

The story

As Formula 1 racing returns to Miami, two prominent restaurants are betting that the Grand Prix weekend can be experienced as much in the dining room and on the rooftop as it is at the track. Amazónico and Selva are introducing a coordinated day-to-night experience that spans race day brunch service, rooftop gathering spaces, and Grand Prix viewing parties, all anchored in Brickell. The initiative represents a shift in how cities and venues approach major motorsport events—treating them not simply as sporting spectacles but as anchors for a broader hospitality ecosystem.

The Miami Grand Prix, established in 2022, has already cemented itself as a premier fixture on the Formula 1 calendar. Held on the first Sunday of May at the Miami International Autodrome near Hard Rock Stadium, the event takes place in the heart of south Florida's most densely populated region. According to Digest Miami, the Amazónico and Selva collaboration offers attendees and Miami residents a structured way to engage with race day activities throughout the day and into the evening, rather than confining the experience to the track or a single venue.

The concept mirrors a broader hospitality trend in major sports cities, where premium restaurants and nightlife venues increasingly capitalize on marquee sporting events by curating themed experiences. The day-to-night model—beginning with a race day brunch and extending through rooftop socializing and evening viewing parties—acknowledges that Formula 1 fans and Miami's affluent demographic may want multiple touchpoints throughout the Grand Prix weekend. Brickell's position as a central business and dining district makes it a logical hub for such an experience, accessible to both downtown Miami residents and visitors staying near the track.

Formula One, the highest class of worldwide open-wheel racing sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA), attracts a global audience and has been considered motorsport's pinnacle since its inaugural championship in 1950. Miami's status as a Grand Prix host elevates the city's profile internationally and creates economic opportunity for local businesses. The Amazónico and Selva partnership extends that opportunity beyond traditional hospitality by embedding fine dining directly into the race weekend narrative.

For venues and organizers, the challenge lies in creating experiences that feel authentic to the high-energy, luxury-focused culture of Formula 1 while remaining accessible and appealing to a broad cross-section of Miami's population. The multi-hour, multi-venue approach—brunch, rooftop, evening viewing—allows for varying levels of engagement and spending, potentially broadening the audience beyond exclusive ticketing tiers. As F1 events become increasingly centralized in urban areas with developed hospitality infrastructure, restaurants and nightlife venues that can quickly adapt their programming to race weekends are likely to see significant revenue upside.
