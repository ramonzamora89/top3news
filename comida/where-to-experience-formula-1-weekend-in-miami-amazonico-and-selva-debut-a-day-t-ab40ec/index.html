Where to Experience Formula 1 Weekend in Miami: Amazónico and Selva Debut a Day-to-Night Race Destination

What's happening

Two Miami restaurants, Amazónico and Selva, are launching a coordinated day-to-night Formula 1 experience during the Miami Grand Prix weekend. The venues will offer race day brunch, rooftop gatherings, and Grand Prix viewing parties in the Brickell neighborhood.

Who's involved

Amazónico and Selva are upscale dining establishments in Miami's Brickell district capitalizing on the Miami Grand Prix, Formula One's premier racing championship held annually since 2022 at the Miami International Autodrome in Miami Gardens.

Why it matters

The partnership demonstrates how major sporting events drive hospitality and entertainment innovation in urban centers, extending the F1 experience beyond the track itself. For Miami's dining scene, it represents a strategy to capture high-spending race weekend visitors and establish venues as lifestyle destinations during marquee events.

The story

Amazónico and Selva are reimagining the Formula 1 Miami Grand Prix weekend with an integrated day-to-night entertainment concept that transforms the race into a lifestyle experience. According to Digest Miami, the two Brickell-based restaurants will host a race day brunch, transition into rooftop gatherings as the afternoon progresses, and culminate in Grand Prix viewing parties for fans seeking premium accommodations during the event.

The Miami Grand Prix itself represents a relatively recent addition to Formula One's global calendar. First held during the 2022 season, the race takes place at the Miami International Autodrome, located around the grounds and facilities of Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, a suburb situated 16 miles north of downtown Miami. The event has become an annual fixture, typically held on the first Sunday of May and immediately following the Kentucky Derby, making it a significant draw for international motorsport enthusiasts and celebrities.

Formula One stands as the highest class of worldwide racing for open-wheel, single-seater formula racing cars, sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile and run by the Formula One Group. Since its inaugural running in 1950, the FIA Formula One World Championship has remained one of the world's premier forms of motorsport and is widely considered the pinnacle of racing competition. The championship's prestige and global viewership make host cities valuable promotional platforms.

The Amazónico and Selva partnership reflects a broader shift in how venues approach major sporting events—no longer content to operate as passive background venues, restaurants and hospitality operators now position themselves as integral components of the event experience. By structuring their offerings around the race day schedule, the establishments create multiple touchpoints for guests, extending engagement from morning through evening and capturing different spending occasions across a single day.

For Miami's hospitality sector, the Grand Prix weekend represents high-stakes commercial opportunity. The race draws wealthy international attendees, corporate groups, and media delegations—demographics that prioritize premium dining and distinctive experiences. The dual-venue strategy employed by Amazónico and Selva allows guests to move seamlessly between locations while maintaining access to curated viewing experiences and elevated service, differentiating these establishments from competitors seeking to capitalize on the same event.
