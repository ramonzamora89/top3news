Where to Experience Formula 1 Weekend in Miami: Amazónico and Selva Debut a Day-to-Night Race Destination

What's happening

Miami hospitality venues Amazónico and Selva are launching a coordinated day-to-night Formula 1 experience during race weekend, featuring a race day brunch, rooftop gathering, and Grand Prix viewing party in Brickell.

Who's involved

Amazónico and Selva are upscale dining and hospitality destinations operating in Miami's Brickell neighborhood. They are capitalizing on Miami's status as a major motorsport hub following the establishment of the Miami Grand Prix.

Why it matters

This move signals how major sporting events drive ancillary tourism and hospitality innovation in host cities. By packaging a full-day experience around Formula 1, these venues are positioning Miami as a destination for premium race weekend entertainment beyond the track itself.

The story

Two of Miami's prominent hospitality venues are reimagining the Formula 1 weekend experience with an integrated day-to-night offering that extends beyond traditional race viewing. Amazónico and Selva are debuting a curated itinerary centered on the Miami Grand Prix, beginning with a race day brunch and flowing into a rooftop gathering before culminating in a Grand Prix viewing party, all anchored in the Brickell neighborhood.

The Miami Grand Prix itself represents a relatively recent addition to Formula One's international calendar. First held during the 2022 season, the race takes place at the Miami International Autodrome, located at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, approximately 16 miles north of downtown Miami. The event is traditionally scheduled for the first Sunday of May, the day after the Kentucky Derby, making it a high-profile fixture on the motorsport calendar. Formula One, sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile and run by the Formula One Group, has been the pinnacle of open-wheel racing since its inception in 1950.

According to Digest Miami, the debut of this day-to-night race destination reflects a broader recognition among Miami hospitality operators that Formula 1 weekends attract affluent visitors seeking premium experiences beyond trackside seats. By clustering brunch, social gathering, and race viewing under a single destination, Amazónico and Selva are offering convenience and continuity for guests navigating the weekend.

The Brickell location is particularly strategic, positioning these venues within the downtown Miami corridor where many race weekend visitors base themselves. The combination of food, beverage, and entertainment aligns with how major sporting events increasingly blur the lines between venue hospitality and broader urban destination marketing.

For the hospitality sector in Miami, the Miami Grand Prix has proven a valuable recurring draw since its 2022 debut. The race weekend generates sustained demand across hotels, restaurants, and entertainment venues during the typically busy early May window. Offerings like those from Amazónico and Selva demonstrate how venues are evolving beyond passive viewership to create experiential packages that justify premium pricing and extend engagement throughout the entire day.
