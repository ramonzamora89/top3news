Where to Experience Formula 1 Weekend in Miami: Amazónico and Selva Debut a Day-to-Night Race Destination

What's happening

Two Miami restaurants, Amazónico and Selva, are launching a coordinated day-to-night Formula 1 experience centered around the Miami Grand Prix, featuring a race day brunch, rooftop gathering, and viewing party in the Brickell neighborhood.

Who's involved

Amazónico and Selva are upscale dining venues operating in Miami's competitive hospitality scene. The Miami Grand Prix itself is a Formula One racing event held annually at the Miami International Autodrome, located at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, which debuted in 2022.

Why it matters

The initiative demonstrates how major sporting events drive ancillary hospitality and entertainment offerings in host cities. By extending the F1 experience beyond the racetrack into rooftop and dining venues, these restaurants capitalize on the influx of spectators and tourists while establishing Miami's identity as a premium motorsport destination.

The story

Miami's dining scene is stepping into the fast lane this Formula 1 season. Amazónico and Selva, two prominent restaurants operating in the city, have created a coordinated day-to-night experience designed to capture the energy of the Miami Grand Prix weekend. The offering spans from a race day brunch through an evening rooftop gathering and into a Grand Prix viewing party, all anchored in the Brickell neighborhood and designed to serve both race attendees and local enthusiasts.

The Miami Grand Prix, which debuted during the 2022 F1 season, has become a marquee event on the Formula One calendar. The race takes place at the Miami International Autodrome, situated at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens, roughly 16 miles north of downtown Miami. Held on the first Sunday of May, the event has established itself as one of the premier forms of motorsport competition, drawing global audiences and transforming Miami into an international racing hub.

According to Digest Miami, the dual-venue activation represents a shift in how restaurants are engaging with major sporting events—moving beyond simple broadcast gatherings to create immersive, multi-hour experiences. The day-to-night format allows patrons to begin their weekend with daytime dining, transition to rooftop social spaces for networking and pre-race anticipation, and culminate in organized viewing parties where the race itself becomes the centerpiece. This structure mirrors broader hospitality trends in premium cities where experiences are increasingly designed around marquee events.

The Brickell location of these offerings is strategic. As Miami's financial district and one of its densest neighborhoods, Brickell attracts both business professionals and tourists. Positioning F1 weekend activities there ensures visibility and accessibility during what is typically one of Miami's busiest tourism periods.

Formula One, sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile and governed by the Formula One Group, remains the highest class of worldwide open-wheel racing since its inaugural 1950 season. The sport's expansion into Miami reflects the city's growing prominence as a global sports and entertainment destination, and partnerships like those created by Amazónico and Selva underscore how local businesses are positioning themselves to benefit from this momentum.
