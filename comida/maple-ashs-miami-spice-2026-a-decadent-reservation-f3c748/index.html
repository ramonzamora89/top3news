Maple & Ash’s Miami Spice 2026, A Decadent Reservation

What's happening

Maple & Ash, a two-star Michelin restaurant in Miami helmed by chef Danny Grant, has unveiled its Miami Spice 2026 dinner menu, showcasing luxe cuisine that blends contemporary and Old World design with premium ingredients and refined service.

Who's involved

Maple & Ash is a high-end restaurant led by two-star Michelin chef and partner Danny Grant. The Michelin Guide, published since 1900 by the French tire company Michelin, awards up to three stars to select restaurants based on anonymous inspector evaluations and serves as an international reference for dining excellence.

Why it matters

The restaurant's participation in Miami Spice 2026 reflects how Michelin-starred establishments are engaging with major culinary events to showcase their caliber. For diners, this represents an opportunity to experience elevated cuisine from a recognized fine-dining destination during a promotional dining period.

The story

Maple & Ash has announced its Miami Spice 2026 dinner menu, reinforcing its position as one of Miami's premier fine-dining destinations. According to Miami Curated, the menu exemplifies the restaurant's commitment to luxury across all dimensions—from the leadership of its two-star Michelin chef and partner Danny Grant to its contemporary-meets-Old World décor and careful sourcing of rich ingredients.

The Michelin Guide, which has been the international standard for restaurant evaluation since its founding in 1900, awards up to three stars to a select few restaurants in certain geographic areas based on assessments by anonymous inspectors. Maple & Ash's two-star status places it among the highest-ranked establishments, a distinction that typically reflects exceptional cuisine, technique, and consistency.

The restaurant's approach to Miami Spice 2026 underscores how Michelin-recognized establishments are leveraging major culinary events to showcase their standards. The menu reflects the kind of careful ingredient selection and professional service that characterize the restaurant's broader operation, suggesting that diners can expect the same rigor during the promotion as they would at regular service.

For the Miami dining public, Maple & Ash's participation in Miami Spice 2026 offers rare access to high-level cuisine at a curated price point. The event provides both returning patrons and newcomers an opportunity to experience Danny Grant's culinary vision and the restaurant's distinctive aesthetic in a special dining context.
