Maple & Ash’s Miami Spice 2026, A Decadent Reservation

What's happening

Maple & Ash Miami is launching its Miami Spice 2026 dinner menu, a special culinary offering from the luxury restaurant helmed by two-Michelin-star chef Danny Grant. The decadent menu promises to showcase the restaurant's signature blend of contemporary and Old World design alongside rich, premium ingredients.

Who's involved

Maple & Ash Miami is led by chef/partner Danny Grant, who holds two Michelin stars—one of the highest honors in fine dining. The Michelin Guide, published by the French tyre company Michelin since 1900, has evolved into an international reference for dining excellence, awarding up to three stars to select restaurants based on anonymous inspector evaluations.

Why it matters

The launch reflects the restaurant's commitment to elevated culinary experiences and its ability to maintain top-tier recognition in Miami's competitive dining scene. Miami Spice offerings typically attract both local diners and tourists seeking high-end cuisine, making this menu a significant moment for the restaurant's profile during the promotional period.

The story

Maple & Ash Miami is rolling out its Miami Spice 2026 dinner menu, a culinary event that underscores the restaurant's position among Miami's most prestigious dining destinations. According to Miami Curated, the menu embodies the luxury and refinement that define the establishment, from its stewardship under two-Michelin-starred chef Danny Grant to its carefully curated service standards.

The restaurant's aesthetic merges contemporary design with Old World sensibilities, creating an environment that complements the sophisticated approach to its cuisine. This design philosophy extends to the ingredients themselves—Maple & Ash prioritizes rich, premium components that reflect both culinary ambition and attention to detail. The two Michelin stars Danny Grant has earned represent the culmination of meticulous work; the Michelin Guide, which has assessed restaurant quality internationally since 1900, awards such recognition only to establishments meeting its rigorous standards for excellence.

Miami Spice, an annual promotional period for Miami's dining community, offers restaurants the opportunity to showcase special menus at prix-fixe pricing. For a restaurant of Maple & Ash's caliber, this represents not merely a promotional vehicle but a chance to demonstrate the depth of its culinary vision. The decadent dinner menu announced for 2026 signals the restaurant's intent to remain a destination for diners seeking world-class cuisine.

The launch of this menu comes at a moment when Miami's fine dining scene continues to attract national attention and investment. Restaurants operating at the highest levels—those recognized by Michelin's star system—face ongoing pressure to innovate while maintaining consistency. Maple & Ash's approach, balancing contemporary creativity with Old World technique and ingredient quality, positions it to meet those demands.

For patrons and industry observers alike, the Miami Spice 2026 offering from Maple & Ash represents an opportunity to experience the restaurant's philosophy at a designated moment. The menu will likely draw both established clientele and newcomers curious about the establishment's acclaim, reinforcing its standing within Miami's luxury dining ecosystem.
