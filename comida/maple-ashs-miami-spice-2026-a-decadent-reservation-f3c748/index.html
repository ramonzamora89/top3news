Maple & Ash’s Miami Spice 2026, A Decadent Reservation

What's happening

Maple & Ash Miami is unveiling a special Miami Spice 2026 dinner menu, showcasing the restaurant's luxury approach to fine dining. The two-Michelin-starred establishment is leveraging the annual Miami Spice promotion to highlight its refined culinary offerings and upscale experience.

Who's involved

Maple & Ash Miami, helmed by two-Michelin-starred chef and partner Danny Grant, is the focal point. The restaurant is known for its blend of contemporary and Old World design, premium ingredients, and professional service standards.

Why it matters

Miami Spice represents a key promotional opportunity for high-end restaurants to attract diners during a competitive period. For Maple & Ash, the event reinforces its position as a luxury dining destination while providing access to its typically exclusive culinary experience at a curated price point.

The story

Maple & Ash Miami is marking Miami Spice 2026 with a decadent dinner menu that exemplifies the restaurant's commitment to luxury dining. According to Miami Curated, the establishment has crafted a special offering that reflects the sophistication and premium quality diners have come to expect from the venue.

The restaurant operates under the stewardship of chef and partner Danny Grant, whose two Michelin stars attest to his culinary excellence and innovation. Michelin, the French tire company's renowned restaurant guide that has evaluated dining establishments since 1900, awards such stars only to restaurants meeting stringent standards for quality and technique. Grant's recognition places Maple & Ash among an elite tier of dining destinations.

Beyond the kitchen, Maple & Ash Miami distinguishes itself through deliberate design choices and service excellence. The venue blends contemporary aesthetics with Old World influences, creating an atmosphere that complements the culinary experience. This attention to every detail—from décor to service professionalism—has established the restaurant as a destination for diners seeking comprehensive luxury experiences.

The Miami Spice 2026 menu represents an opportunity for the restaurant to showcase its rich ingredient selections and refined technique during the annual promotional period. Miami Spice has become an established platform for high-end establishments to attract new clientele while maintaining the caliber expected of their regular offerings.

For Danny Grant and his team, this year's participation continues a tradition of elevating Miami's dining landscape. The special menu demonstrates how restaurants at the highest level adapt to promotional opportunities without compromising the standards that earned their accolades.
