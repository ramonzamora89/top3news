Maple & Ash’s Miami Spice 2026, A Decadent Reservation

What's happening

Maple & Ash, a two-star Michelin restaurant in Miami helmed by chef Danny Grant, has unveiled its Miami Spice 2026 dinner menu, featuring luxury ingredients and refined contemporary-Old World cuisine. The special promotional menu represents the restaurant's participation in Miami's annual Spice dining event.

Who's involved

Maple & Ash is a luxury restaurant in Miami led by two-star Michelin chef and partner Danny Grant. The restaurant is known for its high-end service, luxe décor blending contemporary and Old World aesthetics, and ingredient-focused menus.

Why it matters

The Miami Spice 2026 menu showcases how fine dining establishments leverage seasonal promotional events to attract diners while maintaining their prestigious culinary standards. For Miami's dining scene, Spice participation by a Michelin-recognized establishment signals the event's significance in the hospitality calendar and reinforces the city's growing reputation as a fine dining destination.

The story

Maple & Ash's announcement of its Miami Spice 2026 dinner menu marks another chapter in the restaurant's commitment to delivering decadent, ingredient-driven cuisine to Miami's luxury dining market. According to Miami Curated, the menu reflects the restaurant's signature approach: elevated contemporary cooking grounded in Old World technique and presentation. Chef Danny Grant, who holds two Michelin stars—a distinction awarded by the international Michelin Guide, which has evaluated restaurants for culinary excellence since 1900—continues to shape the restaurant's identity through menus that prioritize rich, premium ingredients and impeccable execution.

The Michelin Guide's star system, which recognizes restaurants in select geographic areas based on anonymous inspector evaluations, remains one of the most prestigious benchmarks in global fine dining. A two-star designation places Maple & Ash among an exclusive group of restaurants deemed to offer excellent cuisine warranting special effort to visit. This standing underscores Grant's culinary vision and the restaurant's operational consistency.

Miami Spice, an annual dining event that invites restaurants across the city to offer prix-fixe menus at accessible price points, has become a marquee opportunity for establishments to showcase their capabilities to broader audiences. By participating with a specially curated menu rather than a simplified offering, Maple & Ash signals confidence in its ability to deliver luxury experiences within the event's parameters. The restaurant's luxe positioning—evident in its contemporary-meets-Old World décor, professional service standards, and ingredient sourcing—shapes how it interprets the Spice format.

The decision to participate in such promotional dining events reflects a broader hospitality strategy: maintaining prestige while engaging with community dining moments. Restaurants of Maple & Ash's caliber use events like Miami Spice to introduce new diners to their kitchens, deepen relationships with existing patrons, and reinforce Miami's position as a serious culinary destination. For diners, the opportunity to access a Michelin-starred kitchen's cuisine through a structured promotional menu offers a particular kind of value proposition.

Miami's dining landscape continues to evolve, with luxury establishments like Maple & Ash anchoring the high end while contributing to the city's overall gastronomic reputation. The restaurant's participation in Miami Spice 2026 demonstrates how fine dining venues integrate into the city's seasonal hospitality rhythms without compromising the standards that earned them their acclaim.
