Maple & Ash’s Miami Spice 2026, A Decadent Reservation

What's happening

Maple & Ash, a two-Michelin-star restaurant in Miami, is offering a luxe tasting menu as part of Miami Spice 2026. The restaurant's executive offerings showcase its commitment to high-end dining with curated ingredients and refined service.

Who's involved

Maple & Ash is led by two-Michelin-star chef and partner Danny Grant, whose stewardship has established the restaurant as a luxury dining destination. The Michelin Guide, published by the French tire company Michelin since 1900, awards up to three stars to select restaurants based on anonymous inspector evaluations, making the two-star designation a marker of exceptional excellence.

Why it matters

The Miami Spice 2026 participation demonstrates how elite fine-dining establishments leverage promotional dining events to attract and retain high-end clientele. For Miami's culinary reputation, having a two-Michelin-star venue participate in such programs signals the city's maturation as a destination for serious gastronomy and sophisticated hospitality.

The story

Maple & Ash in Miami is bringing elevated dining to the 2026 edition of Miami Spice with a decadent reservation-based tasting menu. According to Miami Curated, the restaurant exemplifies luxury dining through multiple dimensions: the stewardship of its two-Michelin-star chef and partner Danny Grant, its contemporary-meets-Old World design aesthetic, and the exceptional quality of its ingredients and service.

The Michelin Guide, an international reference for dining excellence published by the French tire company Michelin since 1900, awards up to three stars to a select few restaurants in certain geographic areas based on evaluations by anonymous inspectors. A two-Michelin-star designation places a restaurant at the highest echelon of recognized culinary achievement, reflecting both technical mastery and consistent excellence across all dimensions of the dining experience.

Miami Spice has traditionally served as a seasonal dining event that allows restaurants to showcase their creativity and attract diners seeking curated tasting experiences. For an establishment like Maple & Ash, participation in such an event represents an opportunity to reinforce its position as a flagship destination within Miami's fine-dining landscape. The restaurant's menu during this period draws on the rich ingredients and sophisticated techniques that define its year-round operations.

The combination of Danny Grant's culinary leadership, the restaurant's carefully considered interior design philosophy, and the commitment to professional service infrastructure creates an environment where high-end tasting menus achieve their intended impact. Diners at Miami Spice 2026 can expect the same standards of excellence that have earned the restaurant its prestigious Michelin recognition.
