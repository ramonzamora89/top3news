Maple & Ash’s Miami Spice 2026, A Decadent Reservation

What's happening

Maple & Ash, the upscale Miami restaurant helmed by two-star Michelin chef Danny Grant, is offering a luxe special dinner menu as part of Miami Spice 2026. The offering showcases the restaurant's hallmark blend of contemporary and Old World design, refined ingredients, and polished service.

Who's involved

Maple & Ash is a fine-dining establishment led by chef/partner Danny Grant, who holds two Michelin stars. The restaurant operates in Miami and is known for its sophisticated approach to cuisine and hospitality.

Why it matters

High-end restaurants participating in Miami Spice—a seasonal promotion designed to drive traffic during slower periods—signal confidence in the luxury dining market and help establish Miami as a premier culinary destination. The participation of a two-Michelin-star establishment elevates the prestige of the promotion and offers diners an accessible entry point to world-class cuisine.

The story

Maple & Ash has unveiled an exceptionally refined dinner menu for Miami Spice 2026, cementing the restaurant's position as one of South Florida's most sought-after fine-dining destinations. The special menu reflects the restaurant's unwavering commitment to luxe hospitality, from the stewardship of its two-star Michelin chef and partner Danny Grant to the contemporary-meets-Old World aesthetic that defines its dining rooms, according to Miami Curated.

The restaurant's participation in Miami Spice represents a strategic move to showcase its capabilities to a broader audience during the annual dining promotion. For diners seeking an entry point into high-end gastronomy, the special menu offers an opportunity to experience the caliber of cooking and service typically associated with top-tier establishments without the full premium pricing of regular à la carte offerings.

Maple & Ash's culinary philosophy centers on rich, carefully sourced ingredients prepared with precision and artistry. The contemporary-meets-Old World design philosophy extends beyond aesthetics into the dining experience itself, where modern techniques meet classical traditions on the plate. This balance has become a hallmark of the restaurant's identity and a key draw for Miami's most discerning diners.

The restaurant's professional service standards have earned it a reputation for hospitality that matches its culinary ambitions. Every aspect of the experience—from the moment guests arrive through their final course—reflects a commitment to excellence that justifies its Michelin recognition and attracts reservations from across the region.

Miami Spice 2026 continues to position Miami as a culinary hub, with restaurants at every level of sophistication participating in the promotion. The inclusion of a two-Michelin-star establishment like Maple & Ash demonstrates the program's ability to attract flagship restaurants and underscores the city's growing status in the national fine-dining conversation.
