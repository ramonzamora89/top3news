Where Chic Meets Flavor: Septimo & Nuna at Four Seasons Miami

What's happening

Four Seasons Miami is showcasing two standout bars—Septimo and Nuna—that exemplify luxury hospitality's approach to craft cocktails and upscale dining. The properties are being recognized for blending sophisticated design, elevated service, and distinctive culinary experiences.

Who's involved

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, a Canadian luxury hotel company headquartered in Toronto, operates more than 130 hotels and resorts worldwide and has been majority-owned since 2007 by Bill Gates and Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal through their respective investment vehicles. Miami Curated, a lifestyle publication focused on the city's premium dining and hospitality scene, is spotlighting these venues.

Why it matters

As luxury hospitality continues to emphasize experiential dining and bar culture, Four Seasons' investment in distinctive beverage and culinary destinations reflects how premium hotel brands are differentiating themselves beyond accommodation. For Miami's upscale market, these venues represent the standard-setting approach to what defines a world-class bar experience in a competitive hospitality landscape.

The story

Miami's luxury hospitality scene has long been defined by its ability to attract discerning travelers seeking not just accommodation but entire lifestyle experiences. Four Seasons Miami exemplifies this philosophy through Septimo and Nuna, two bars that have become focal points for understanding what separates merely good establishments from truly exceptional ones. According to Miami Curated, the hallmark of an exceptional bar lies in a deliberate combination of factors: an atmosphere that feels simultaneously chic and welcoming, service that anticipates rather than merely responds to needs, and the kind of thoughtful details that transform a visit into a memorable occasion.

Septimo and Nuna each bring distinct identities to Four Seasons Miami's food and beverage portfolio. Rather than serving as generic hotel bars, they function as destination venues in their own right, drawing locals and hotel guests alike. The emphasis on both visual sophistication and flavor craftsmanship reflects a broader shift within luxury hospitality, where bars have evolved beyond their traditional role to become architectural and culinary statements about a property's values.

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, as a Canadian company operating more than 130 properties worldwide, has long built its reputation on consistency of quality while allowing individual properties to develop local character. The company's ownership structure—with Bill Gates and Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal holding majority stakes through their respective investment vehicles since 2007—ensures access to substantial capital for the kind of design and operational investments that create standout venues. This ownership model has enabled Four Seasons to maintain its luxury positioning while investing in experiences that go beyond standard hotel offerings.

The recognition of Septimo and Nuna speaks to Miami's maturation as a luxury destination. Rather than relying solely on beachfront appeal or nightlife reputation, the city's premier venues increasingly compete on sophistication, service caliber, and culinary innovation. For Four Seasons Miami specifically, these bars serve as anchors for guest experience and as attractions that can influence a traveler's choice of where to stay in a crowded market.

What distinguishes these establishments is their refusal to compromise on either aesthetics or substance. The combination of chic design, careful attention to service details, and quality in the glass reflects an understanding that luxury hospitality today requires excellence across every dimension of the guest experience—from the moment of arrival through each interaction, down to the final details of how a drink is presented and crafted.
