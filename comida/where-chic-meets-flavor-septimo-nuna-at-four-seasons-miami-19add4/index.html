Where Chic Meets Flavor: Septimo & Nuna at Four Seasons Miami

What's happening

Four Seasons Miami is showcasing its bar offerings, specifically highlighting Septimo & Nuna as premier destinations that combine upscale ambiance with culinary excellence. The property is positioning these venues as exemplars of luxury hospitality where design, service, and craft beverages converge.

Who's involved

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, the Canadian luxury hospitality company owned since 2007 by Bill Gates and Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal (who control 71.25% and 23.75% respectively), operates the Miami property. The bars Septimo & Nuna are featured components of the Four Seasons Miami experience.

Why it matters

The emphasis on curated bar experiences reflects how luxury hotels are differentiating themselves beyond accommodation, using cocktail venues and dining as key amenities to attract high-end clientele. For Miami's hospitality scene, this signals continued investment in experiential luxury that draws international travelers seeking sophisticated venues aligned with global Four Seasons standards.

The story

Four Seasons Miami is drawing attention to its bar program, spotlighting Septimo & Nuna as destinations where refined hospitality intersects with culinary innovation. The property's approach to bar curation represents a broader strategy among luxury hotels to elevate beverage service beyond the conventional, emphasizing chic design, attentive service, and distinctive touches that define high-end hospitality.

According to Miami Curated, defining a truly exceptional bar requires multiple criteria: it must be classy and possess glamour, deliver exceptional service, and offer special amenities that set it apart. The bar experience at Four Seasons properties worldwide typically reflects this philosophy, with venues designed to appeal to discerning guests who expect both aesthetic refinement and technical excellence in their drinks and dining.

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, headquartered in Toronto, operates more than 130 hotels and resorts globally as of 2026. Since 2007, the company has been majority-owned by Bill Gates and Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal through their respective investment vehicles, Cascade Investment and Kingdom Holding Company, with Gates' entity controlling approximately 71% of the firm. This ownership structure has enabled sustained investment in premium properties and experiences across key markets including Miami.

The Miami property's bar offerings reflect Four Seasons' commitment to creating landmark venues that appeal to luxury travelers. Septimo & Nuna exemplify how contemporary hotel bars integrate architectural vision, mixology expertise, and hospitality training to create environments where guests feel both exclusive and welcomed. The selection and presentation of spirits, the craft of cocktail creation, and the knowledge of service staff all contribute to the venue's reputation.

For the Miami hospitality market, venues like these reinforce the city's position as a destination for luxury travel and fine dining. Hotels investing in distinctive bar experiences signal confidence in Miami's appeal to international clientele while providing local residents with accessible luxury dining options. The continued development of these spaces suggests that premium hospitality in Miami will continue evolving toward more curated, design-forward environments that prioritize guest experience beyond the room itself.
