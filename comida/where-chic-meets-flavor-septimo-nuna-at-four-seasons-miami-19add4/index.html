Where Chic Meets Flavor: Septimo & Nuna at Four Seasons Miami

What's happening

Four Seasons Miami is showcasing two standout bars—Septimo and Nuna—that blend sophisticated design with culinary excellence. The properties are being highlighted as exemplars of what defines a world-class bar experience in Miami's competitive hospitality landscape.

Who's involved

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, a Canadian luxury hotel company with over 130 properties worldwide, operates the Miami location featuring these bars. The company has been majority-owned since 2007 by Bill Gates and Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal, with Gates-controlled Cascade Investment holding 71.25% stake as of January 2022.

Why it matters

The recognition of these venues reflects the elevated standards luxury hospitality brands maintain to differentiate themselves in premium markets. For Miami's dining and hospitality sector, such high-profile endorsements from established luxury operators help define contemporary expectations for bar design, service quality, and culinary innovation.

The story

Four Seasons Miami is drawing attention to two of its signature bar offerings—Septimo and Nuna—that exemplify the intersection of refined aesthetics and exceptional flavor craftsmanship. According to Miami Curated, the properties succeed by meeting a distinct set of criteria: they maintain a classy, chic atmosphere with genuine glamour, deliver exceptional service, and incorporate thoughtful amenities that elevate the guest experience beyond standard hospitality.

The bars represent the kind of venues typically associated with the world's finest landmark hotels. Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, the Canadian luxury hotel company, operates more than 130 properties globally and has maintained its reputation for curated experiences through careful attention to detail in every aspect of guest interaction. With majority ownership held since 2007 by Bill Gates and Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal—Cascade Investment controls 71.25% and Kingdom Holding Company controls 23.75% as of January 2022—the company has resources to invest in distinctive venue design and staffing.

Septimo and Nuna each bring their own identity to the Four Seasons Miami portfolio. These venues appear designed to serve guests who view a bar not merely as a place to drink, but as a destination experience. The emphasis on "special touches" and deliberate amenities suggests attention to the sensory and social dimensions of hospitality—from cocktail craftsmanship to ambiance to the caliber of personal service.

Miami's bar scene has become increasingly competitive, with establishments vying for recognition among both locals and travelers seeking premium experiences. The Four Seasons' investment in these two distinct venues reflects a broader understanding within luxury hospitality that modern guests expect multiple tiers of experience within a single property. Rather than a single signature bar, the strategy of offering Septimo and Nuna allows the hotel to cater to different moods and occasions while maintaining consistent standards of quality and design.

The recognition of these bars speaks to how contemporary luxury hospitality distinguishes itself through curation and intentionality. For Four Seasons, the formula combines architectural and design sophistication, trained staff capable of personalized service, and culinary ambition in every drink and dining offering. In a market as crowded as Miami, such focused excellence becomes the baseline expectation among the clientele these properties target.
