Where Chic Meets Flavor: Septimo & Nuna at Four Seasons Miami

What's happening

Four Seasons Miami is showcasing its bar offerings, specifically Septimo & Nuna, as exemplars of luxury hospitality design and service. The property positions these venues as destinations that blend sophistication with culinary excellence for discerning travelers.

Who's involved

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, a Canadian luxury hotel company owned majority by Bill Gates' Cascade Investment (71.25%) and Prince Al-Waleed bin Talal's Kingdom Holding Company (23.75%) since 2007, operates the Miami property. Miami Curated, a local lifestyle publication, is evaluating the bars as part of a broader assessment of the city's premium nightlife venues.

Why it matters

The feature reflects how luxury hotel groups differentiate themselves in competitive markets through experiential amenities beyond accommodations. For travelers and locals seeking high-end venues, identifying bars that combine aesthetic refinement with hospitality standards signals where to find consistently excellent service and design.

The story

Four Seasons Miami is drawing attention to its bar concepts, Septimo & Nuna, as the property reinforces its position among the world's elite hospitality destinations. According to Miami Curated, the evaluation of the city's best bars hinges on criteria that extend beyond drinks—classiness, chic design, glamour, and what the publication terms special "amenities" distinguish truly exceptional venues from ordinary ones.

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts operates more than 130 hotels and resorts worldwide as of 2026, each maintaining the brand's hallmark standards for luxury and service. The company, headquartered in Toronto and majority-owned by prominent investors since 2007, has built its reputation on delivering experiences that transcend typical hospitality. The Miami property exemplifies this approach by curating bar environments that reflect both architectural sophistication and culinary innovation.

The emphasis on bar quality within luxury hotels reflects a broader shift in how premium properties compete. Rather than treating bars as secondary amenities, Four Seasons Miami positions Septimo & Nuna as destination venues in their own right—places where guests and locals alike can experience the refined service standards and attention to detail that define luxury hospitality. The design and service philosophy apparent in these bars mirrors the experience found in landmark properties across the world's most prestigious hotel portfolios.

For Miami's hospitality landscape, such investment in bar concepts matters because it establishes benchmarks for quality and design that influence the broader market. Properties that dedicate resources to creating distinctive bar environments signal their commitment to comprehensive luxury—acknowledging that modern travelers and residents value experiences that integrate environment, service, and craft equally. The Four Seasons Miami example suggests that exceptional bars have become integral to how luxury hotels maintain competitive advantage and brand loyalty.
