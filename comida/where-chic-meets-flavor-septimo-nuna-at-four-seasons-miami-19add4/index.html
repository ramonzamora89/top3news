Where Chic Meets Flavor: Septimo & Nuna at Four Seasons Miami

What's happening

Four Seasons Miami is showcasing its bar offerings, specifically highlighting Septimo & Nuna as premier destinations that blend sophisticated design with culinary excellence. The property is positioning itself as a top choice for discerning Miami visitors seeking elevated nightlife experiences.

Who's involved

Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, the Canadian luxury hospitality company operating more than 130 properties worldwide, is behind the Miami property and its bar concepts. The feature comes from Miami Curated, a local lifestyle publication focused on the city's dining and entertainment scene.

Why it matters

As luxury travel and experiential hospitality continue to drive high-end tourism, bars at flagship properties like Four Seasons have become destination venues in their own right. The emphasis on design, service, and ambiance reflects how luxury brands are competing for affluent consumers who view venues as cultural destinations rather than mere functional spaces.

The story

Four Seasons Miami is drawing attention to its bar program through features highlighting Septimo & Nuna, venues that exemplify the property's approach to elevated hospitality. According to Miami Curated, defining the best bars requires multiple criteria—but classiness, chic design, stellar service, and special amenities consistently distinguish the world's finest establishments.

The Four Seasons brand operates in a rarefied segment of global hospitality. As of January 2022, the Canadian luxury company—which operates more than 130 hotels and resorts worldwide—is majority-owned by Gates-controlled Cascade Investment, which holds 71.25% of the company, with Kingdom Holding Company controlling 23.75%. This ownership structure underscores the resources and standards applied across the portfolio.

The Miami property's bars reflect a broader shift in how luxury hospitality companies approach F&B venues. Rather than viewing bars as secondary revenue streams, Four Seasons has positioned them as signature experiences that define a property's identity. Septimo & Nuna's inclusion in curated features signals that these spaces meet the exacting standards expected of a flagship Four Seasons property: thoughtful design, curated beverage programs, and service calibrated to high-net-worth clientele.

Miami's competitive hospitality landscape has intensified in recent years, with properties vying for the attention of affluent travelers and local residents alike. Bars that combine glamour with genuine hospitality—rather than relying on hype alone—have emerged as differentiators. The emphasis on amenities and special touches reflects what discerning guests increasingly expect: venues that feel exclusive without sacrificing warmth or professionalism.

The feature on Septimo & Nuna also speaks to Miami's evolution as a destination where design, food culture, and nightlife intersect. Four Seasons' positioning of its bar program as a draw in its own right acknowledges that luxury consumers often choose properties based on their dining and drinking experiences. For the property, this strategy reinforces its market position among the city's most demanding visitors.
