DC Pie Co. and Apocalypse BBQ Bring Pizza and Barbecue Together for National BBQ Day in Miami

What's happening

DC Pie Co. and Apocalypse BBQ have launched a limited-edition collaborative pizza called The Smokeshow Pie, available in Miami through May. The partnership celebrates National BBQ Day by merging two beloved cuisines—pizza and barbecue—into a single offering.

Who's involved

DC Pie Co. is a pizzeria known for craft pies, while Apocalypse BBQ brings expertise in barbecue preparation. Together, they're combining their respective culinary strengths to create a fusion product for the Miami market.

Why it matters

Food collaborations between established local brands can drive traffic to both businesses and generate buzz around limited-time offerings, encouraging customers to visit before the promotion ends. This merger of pizza and barbecue also reflects a broader consumer appetite for innovative fusion dishes that blend distinct flavor profiles and cooking traditions.

The story

DC Pie Co. and Apocalypse BBQ are joining forces this spring to introduce The Smokeshow Pie, a limited-edition pizza that marries two American culinary favorites. The collaborative offering is now available in Miami and will remain on menus through May, giving diners a window to experience the fusion creation before it disappears.

The partnership represents a strategic convergence of two well-regarded local brands. DC Pie Co. brings its reputation for carefully crafted pizzas, while Apocalypse BBQ contributes its expertise in smoke, spice, and slow-cooked meats. By combining their strengths, the restaurants aim to deliver a product greater than the sum of its parts—a pizza that leverages barbecue's bold, smoky character without abandoning pizza's accessible appeal.

According to Digest Miami, the collaboration was conceived specifically around National BBQ Day, a natural tie-in that positions the offering as both timely and thematic. The limited-time structure creates urgency; customers who want to try The Smokeshow Pie must act before May ends, a marketing tactic that has proven effective for restaurants seeking to boost traffic and test menu innovation with lower risk.

Fusion cuisine and limited-edition items have become powerful tools for restaurants to differentiate themselves in competitive markets. By offering something genuinely novel rather than simply extending existing menus, DC Pie Co. and Apocalypse BBQ are positioning themselves as willing to experiment and collaborate—qualities that resonate with diners seeking fresh culinary experiences.

The initiative also highlights Miami's vibrant food scene, where diverse restaurants and cuisines coexist and intersect. Collaborations like this one help establish the city as a place where chefs and owners actively engage with one another, creating opportunities for cross-pollination and shared customer bases.
