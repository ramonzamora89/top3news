‘The Devil’s Mouth’ Director Breaks Down His Bloody, One-Location Shark Movie, Those ‘Demanding’ Stunts and Why Kathryn Newton and Lana Condor Wanted to Switch Roles

What's happening

Director Jeff Wadlow is discussing the making of 'The Devil's Mouth,' a physically intense shark thriller shot in a single location. The film stars Kathryn Newton and Lana Condor, who were so committed to the roles they considered switching parts during production.

Who's involved

Jeff Wadlow directs the project, while actresses Kathryn Newton and Lana Condor headline the cast. Newton is known for 'Paranormal Activity 4' and 'Pokémon Detective Pikachu,' while Condor gained prominence through 'X-Men: Apocalypse' and 'To All the Boys I've Loved Before.'

Why it matters

The production demonstrates how modern action films demand extreme physical commitment from actors, setting expectations for the thriller genre. The actors' willingness to switch roles shows the collaborative and physically rigorous nature of contemporary filmmaking.

More context

'The Devil's Mouth' follows a growing trend of confined-location thrillers that maximize tension through limited settings—similar to recent hits like 'The Shallows.' The film's emphasis on underwater stunt work and practical effects reflects an industry movement away from pure CGI toward hybrid approaches. Shark movies have seen renewed audience interest following the success of films like 'The Meg' and survival content's popularity on streaming platforms. Wadlow previously directed action films like 'Kick-Ass 2' and 'Never Back Down,' establishing his expertise in physically demanding productions.
