Oscar Nominee Barkhad Abdi, Jim Deddes Lead Teddy Cherim’s ‘Maalstroom’; First-Look Image Released (EXCLUSIVE)

What's happening

Dutch-Belgian film 'Maalstroom' has wrapped production with a cast led by Oscar-nominated actor Barkhad Abdi alongside Jim Deddes and Tenzing Woing. Variety has released the first exclusive look-image from the project.

Who's involved

Barkhad Abdi is a Somali-American actor best known for his Oscar-nominated role as pirate Abduwali Muse in the 2013 film 'Captain Phillips,' which also earned him a BAFTA and Golden Globe nomination. The film is written and directed by Teddy Cherim and is a co-production between Dutch and Belgian producers.

Why it matters

The project marks another major film role for Abdi following his breakthrough performance in 'Captain Phillips,' continuing his trajectory in international cinema. The completion of 'Maalstroom' signals the emergence of cross-European co-productions with internationally recognized talent, positioning the film for potential festival and distribution attention.
