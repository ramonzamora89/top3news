Marvel’s ‘X-Men’ Reboot Sets May 2028 Release Date

What's happening

Marvel Studios has officially announced that an X-Men reboot will arrive in the MCU on May 5, 2028. The film will star Sadie Sink as Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Cyclops, and Samara Weaving as Emma Frost, among others.

Who's involved

Marvel Studios, the superhero film production company owned by Disney, is spearheading the project under the leadership of executive Kevin Feige. The X-Men themselves are a superhero team created by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby that first appeared in 1963 and have become one of Marvel Comics's most recognizable franchises.

Why it matters

This announcement marks a major milestone in integrating the X-Men into the MCU's shared universe, a franchise that has dominated global box office and pop culture for over a decade. The 2028 release date provides clarity on Marvel's long-term slate and signals the company's commitment to bringing one of comics' most celebrated teams into its interconnected film ecosystem.

The story

Marvel Studios has set an official release date for its long-awaited X-Men reboot, confirming that the film will arrive on May 5, 2028. According to Variety, Kevin Feige, a key executive at Marvel Studios, made the announcement confirming the project's place in the studio's future lineup. The cast announced includes Sadie Sink in the role of Jean Grey, Kit Connor as Cyclops, and Samara Weaving as Emma Frost.

The X-Men have a storied history in American comic books, having been created by writer and editor Stan Lee alongside artist Jack Kirby. The team first appeared in The X-Men #1 and quickly became a cornerstone of Marvel Comics' output. Though the original series was cancelled in 1970 due to low sales, a 1975 revival under writer Chris Claremont transformed the franchise into one of Marvel Comics's most recognizable and commercially successful properties.

Bringing the X-Men into the Marvel Cinematic Universe represents a significant expansion of the MCU's roster of characters and teams. The MCU itself is an American media franchise centered on superhero films produced by Marvel Studios and owned by the Walt Disney Company. Since its establishment through interconnected plot elements and shared settings, the MCU has become a dominant force in global entertainment, spanning films, television series, and digital content.

The 2028 release date gives Marvel Studios and its creative teams nearly four years to develop the film, allowing time for script refinement, production design, and post-production work on what is expected to be a major tentpole release. The inclusion of established cast members signals the studio's intent to bring substantial star power to the project.

For Marvel fans, this announcement fulfills a long-standing anticipation around how the studio would integrate the mutant heroes into its shared universe. The X-Men's arrival in the MCU opens new narrative possibilities and character interactions that could reshape the franchise's direction for years to come.
