Jay Baruchel Is ‘The Stunt Driver’ in New Stills as IFC Comedy-Drama Heads to World Premiere at Toronto

What's happening

Jay Baruchel stars in "The Stunt Driver," a new comedy-drama that is premiering at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 10. The Independent Film Company acquired the film in July and will give it a theatrical release on September 25.

Who's involved

Jay Baruchel is a Canadian actor and filmmaker known for voicing Hiccup in the How to Train Your Dragon franchise and starring in comedies like She's Out of My League and This Is the End. The Independent Film Company is the distributor behind the project's acquisition and upcoming theatrical rollout.

Why it matters

The film's world premiere at one of the world's most prestigious film festivals signals industry confidence in the project and gives it high-profile exposure before its wide theatrical release. For Baruchel, whose films have grossed over $1.28 billion worldwide, the role marks another significant project in his acting and filmmaking career.

The story

Jay Baruchel is taking center stage in "The Stunt Driver," a new comedy-drama that will make its world premiere at the Toronto International Film Festival on September 10. The Independent Film Company, which acquired the film on July 21, has released fresh stills showing Baruchel in the lead role, building anticipation ahead of the prestigious festival debut. The company plans to bring the film to theaters on September 25, following its Toronto unveiling.

Baruchel, a Canadian actor and filmmaker, has built a formidable career spanning both comedy and voice acting work. He is best known for voicing Hiccup Haddock in the How to Train Your Dragon franchise from 2010 to 2019, a role that helped establish him as a major presence in family entertainment. Beyond animation, Baruchel has anchored comedies including Fanboys, She's Out of My League, and This Is the End, demonstrating his range in live-action comedy.

According to Variety, the acquisition and theatrical release strategy reflect the Independent Film Company's confidence in "The Stunt Driver" as a commercial and critical prospect. The timing of the Toronto premiere ahead of the September 25 theatrical date allows the film to benefit from festival buzz and press coverage, a traditional strategy for independent releases seeking wider audience reach.

Baruchel's involvement in the project comes as his filmography continues to generate substantial box office returns. Films in which he has had starring roles have grossed over $1.28 billion at the worldwide box office as of 2024, indicating his proven appeal to audiences across multiple genres and demographics. His participation in "The Stunt Driver" positions the film to tap into his established fanbase while introducing it to new viewers.

The Toronto International Film Festival, one of the world's largest and most prestigious publicly attended film festivals, provides an ideal platform for a comedy-drama seeking to establish itself in the marketplace. The festival's prominence means that the September 10 premiere will draw media attention and industry scrutiny, setting the tone for the film's theatrical run two weeks later.
