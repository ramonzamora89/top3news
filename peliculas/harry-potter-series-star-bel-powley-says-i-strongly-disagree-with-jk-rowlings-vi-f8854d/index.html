‘Harry Potter’ Series Star Bel Powley Says ‘I Strongly Disagree With J.K. Rowling’s Views on Gender’: ‘The Trans Community Deserve Safety and Dignity’

What's happening

Bel Powley, cast as Petunia Dursley in HBO's upcoming Harry Potter TV adaptation, has publicly stated she strongly disagrees with author J.K. Rowling's views on gender and is affirming support for the transgender community.

Who's involved

Bel Powley is an actress set to play Petunia Dursley in HBO's adaptation of the Harry Potter series, the best-selling book franchise in history with over 600 million copies sold worldwide. J.K. Rowling is the British author behind the seven-volume Harry Potter series and has faced sustained controversy over her public statements regarding gender and sex.

Why it matters

The statement highlights ongoing tensions between the creative legacy of the Harry Potter franchise and its author's publicly stated positions on gender identity, an issue that has divided cast members and fans since Rowling's statements began circulating years ago. As major productions like HBO's new adaptation move forward, cast members' willingness to openly distance themselves from the author signals the enduring nature of this cultural divide within the franchise's fanbase and industry.

The story

Actress Bel Powley has become the latest member of the Harry Potter universe to publicly distance herself from author J.K. Rowling's stance on gender issues. Powley, who will portray Petunia Dursley in HBO's highly anticipated television adaptation of the beloved book series, stated that she "strongly disagrees" with Rowling's views and emphasized that "the trans community deserve safety and dignity," according to Variety.

Rowling's positions on gender and sex have sparked significant controversy in recent years, drawing criticism from LGBTQ+ advocates and some cast and crew members associated with the franchise. The author, whose Harry Potter series became the best-selling book series in history with over 600 million copies sold globally, has maintained her stance on these issues despite the backlash. Her comments have created a notable divide between the cultural phenomenon she created and portions of its fanbase.

HBO's new Harry Potter television adaptation represents a major expansion of the franchise, which has already generated enormous revenue through the eight-film series produced by Warner Bros. Pictures from 2001 to 2011, along with the Fantastic Beasts prequel films beginning in 2016. As production moves forward on this new iteration, statements like Powley's reflect the ongoing challenge facing major productions tied to Rowling's work: navigating the author's influence on the material while addressing her public positions that some cast members find objectionable.

Powley's comments add to a pattern of Harry Potter actors and creators openly disagreeing with or condemning Rowling's statements. The franchise's cultural significance—spanning multiple generations of readers and viewers—has made these tensions particularly visible and consequential for the industry and fanbase alike. HBO's casting of Powley and her subsequent statement suggest the network is proceeding with the adaptation even amid these ongoing controversies.

The tension between Rowling's literary creation and her public persona presents a unique challenge for the entertainment industry. As beloved franchises navigate complex social and political landscapes, the question of how casts and creators engage with controversial figures behind the material continues to evolve, with performers increasingly using their platforms to articulate their own values.
