Raima Sen, Shrimoyee Chakraborty, Aarti Bajaj Team on Parno Mittra’s Producing Debut ‘The Music Teacher’ (EXCLUSIVE)

What's happening

Parno Mittra, an acclaimed Bengali actress, is making her producing debut with 'The Music Teacher,' a British-Indian short film starring fellow actor Raima Sen. Principal photography is set to begin this month in Kolkata.

Who's involved

Parno Mittra is a Bengali film and television actress who rose to prominence after her role in the National Award-winning film 'Ranjana Ami Ar Ashbona' (2011). Raima Sen is a respected Indian actress known for her work in Bengali and Hindi cinema, with acclaimed performances in films like 'Chokher Bali,' 'Parineeta,' and 'Baishe Srabon.'

Why it matters

The project marks a significant career milestone for Mittra as she transitions into film production, expanding her creative footprint beyond acting. For the Bengali film industry, it represents continued cross-cultural collaboration, blending British and Indian storytelling perspectives on screen.

The story

Parno Mittra, an accomplished Bengali cinema actress, is stepping into a new creative role as producer on her debut project 'The Music Teacher,' a British-Indian short film that will star fellow acclaimed actor Raima Sen. According to Variety, principal photography is scheduled to begin this month in Kolkata, with the production co-produced by Tabernacle.

Mittra's transition behind the camera marks a notable career expansion for an actress who has built a reputation across Bengali film and television. She began her entertainment career on television with a role in Ravi Ojha's Bengali TV series 'Khela' in 2007, before achieving wider recognition after her performance in Anjan Dutta's National Award-winning film 'Ranjana Ami Ar Ashbona' (2011). That breakthrough role catapulted her to stardom within Bengali cinema.

Raima Sen, who headlines 'The Music Teacher,' brings her own distinguished resume to the project. Known for her natural performances across Bengali and Hindi films, Sen has earned critical acclaim for roles in notable productions including 'Chokher Bali,' 'Parineeta,' 'The Japanese Wife,' and 'Baishe Srabon.' Her involvement signals the caliber of talent Mittra has assembled for her producing debut.

The short film's British-Indian designation suggests a cross-cultural narrative approach, reflecting the increasing collaboration between Indian filmmakers and international production partners. The choice to film in Kolkata grounds the project in the heart of Bengali cinema culture, where both Mittra and Sen have established their strongest roots.

For Mittra, the move into production represents a natural evolution of her creative ambitions, building on years of on-screen experience to shape stories from behind the camera. The involvement of established actors like Sen alongside new collaborators suggests an ambitious artistic vision for the project. As production gets underway, 'The Music Teacher' stands as the latest example of Bengali cinema's continued growth and evolving creative collaborations.
