Bella Thorne and Zendaya Got Pitted Against Each Other During Disney Fame and Snuck Off Set to ‘Hash It Out’ After Things ‘Got Pretty Bad’

What's happening

Bella Thorne has revealed that she and Zendaya were deliberately pitted against each other during their time as Disney Channel stars, leading to tension so severe that the two actors left the set to resolve their conflict privately.

Who's involved

Bella Thorne and Zendaya are both American actresses who rose to prominence through Disney Channel programming. Zendaya has since become a major entertainment figure, earning two Primetime Emmy Awards and a Golden Globe, and was named one of Time's 100 most influential people in 2022.

Why it matters

The revelation exposes internal industry dynamics at Disney during the peak of the channel's cultural dominance, showing how network practices may have fueled rivalry among young talent. It contributes to broader conversations about how child and young adult performers are managed and positioned against one another for competitive advantage.

The story

Bella Thorne opened up on the latest episode of the "Call Her Daddy" podcast about a difficult chapter in her relationship with her former Disney co-star Zendaya, revealing that the two were aggressively pitted against each other during the height of their stardom on the network. The tension became so pronounced that Thorne and Zendaya eventually felt compelled to leave the set and address their conflict privately, with Thorne describing the situation as having "gotten pretty bad." According to Variety, the two actors became household names among Disney Channel viewers for playing CeCe in their shared project, "Shake It Off."

The practice of creating artificial competition between young talent was evidently deliberate on Disney's part, a strategy that appears to have damaged the working relationship between the two rising stars at a critical moment in their careers. Thorne's decision to publicly discuss this dynamic years later speaks to the lasting impact such behind-the-scenes maneuvering can have on performers, particularly those navigating the pressures of early fame while still developing professionally.

Zendaya has gone on to become one of the entertainment industry's most decorated and influential figures, with her career spanning Emmy-winning performances and recognition from institutions like Time magazine. Thorne, who began her rise to prominence through earlier roles in series like "Dirty Sexy Money" and "My Own Worst Enemy," has built a multifaceted career as an actress, singer, and writer. The revelation suggests that despite their eventual professional separation, the period of manufactured rivalry left an impression significant enough for Thorne to address on a major platform.

The anecdote highlights a rarely discussed aspect of the Disney Channel's operational methods during its peak influence in youth entertainment. While the network became famous for discovering and launching the careers of numerous young performers, this account suggests that competition between talent may have sometimes been actively encouraged or orchestrated, rather than emerging organically from market forces.
