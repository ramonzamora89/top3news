‘One Battle After Another’ and ‘Marty Supreme’ Take Top Honors at Location Managers Guild International Awards

What's happening

The Location Managers Guild International held its annual awards ceremony on Saturday night, with "One Battle After Another" and "Marty Supreme" emerging as the top winners. The LMGI Awards celebrate excellence in the selection and use of filming locations across film, television, and commercials.

Who's involved

The Location Managers Guild International is a professional organization representing location managers, scouts, assistant location managers, and affiliated industry members such as film commissions and location services vendors. "Marty Supreme" is a 2025 sports comedy-drama directed by Josh Safdie, starring Timothée Chalamet as a 1950s table tennis player pursuing a world championship.

Why it matters

Location scouting and management are critical but often overlooked components of film production that directly impact a project's visual identity, budget, and logistical feasibility. Recognition from the LMGI elevates the profile of this specialized craft and signals to the industry which productions have successfully leveraged their locations as a creative asset.

The story

"One Battle After Another" and "Marty Supreme" captured the top honors at the Location Managers Guild International Awards, which were presented on Saturday night. The ceremony celebrates the artistry and strategic importance of location selection across motion pictures, television, and commercials—work that shapes how stories are told and where they come to life.

The Location Managers Guild International represents a diverse professional community including location managers, location scouts, assistant location managers, and affiliated business members such as film commissions, location services companies, and filming venues. According to Variety, the LMGI Awards recognize exceptional achievement in the selection and use of filming locations. The guild's membership spans the industry, underscoring how location management touches nearly every production made today.

"Marty Supreme," directed by Josh Safdie and co-written with Ronald Bronstein, tells the story of Marty Mauser, a 1950s table tennis player driven to achieve world championship status. Timothée Chalamet stars in the lead role, with supporting performances from Gwyneth Paltrow, Odessa A'zion, Kevin O'Leary, Tyler Okonma, Abel Ferrara, and Fran Drescher. The film's setting in the 1950s and its focus on the specialized world of competitive table tennis likely demanded careful location scouting to authentically capture the era and atmosphere.

The choice of venues and locations serves as more than mere backdrop in contemporary filmmaking. A skilled location manager must balance creative vision with practical constraints—finding spaces that match a director's aesthetic while accommodating crews, equipment, and budgets. The LMGI Awards highlight this often-invisible labor, bringing recognition to the professionals who enable filmmakers to realize their stories in real spaces.

The recognition of both "One Battle After Another" and "Marty Supreme" reflects the guild's commitment to honoring diverse achievements across the medium. These awards validate location management as a craft worthy of industry attention and celebrate the locations themselves as creative collaborators in the filmmaking process.
