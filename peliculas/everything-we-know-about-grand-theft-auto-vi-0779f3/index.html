Everything We Know About ‘Grand Theft Auto VI’

What's happening

Grand Theft Auto VI is launching on November 19, more than a decade after the previous installment in the iconic series. Players will step into the roles of Jason and Lucia, protagonists facing a stacked deck in what Rockstar Games describes as another entry in the action-adventure franchise.

Who's involved

Rockstar Games, the American video game publisher based in New York City and a subsidiary of Take-Two Interactive since 1998, is publishing the title. The game is developed by Rockstar North, the British development house that has helmed the series under the creative oversight of brothers Dan and Sam Houser, with Sam Houser currently heading the studio as president.

Why it matters

GTA VI's release marks a major cultural and commercial moment after a 12-year gap, potentially setting new standards for the action-adventure genre and generating significant revenue for Take-Two Interactive. The game's launch will be closely watched by the industry as a bellwether for player appetite and technological capabilities in open-world game design.

The story

Grand Theft Auto VI is finally arriving on November 19, ending a wait that has lasted more than a decade since the last mainline entry in the series. According to Variety, the game will center on two protagonists named Jason and Lucia, characters described by publisher Rockstar Games as individuals who "have always known the deck is stacked against them."

Rockstar Games, established in December 1998 as a subsidiary of Take-Two Interactive, has built the Grand Theft Auto franchise into one of gaming's most recognizable and commercially successful properties. The series, created by David Jones and Mike Dailly, is primarily developed by Rockstar North, a British development house that operates under the creative direction of brothers Dan and Sam Houser, who previously held executive positions at BMG Interactive before joining Take-Two. Sam Houser currently serves as president of the studio.

The 12-year development cycle reflects the scale and ambition of modern triple-A game production. The previous mainline entry, Grand Theft Auto V, released in 2013 and became one of the best-selling video games of all time, establishing an enormous baseline of expectations for its successor. GTA VI will need to justify its lengthy development period by delivering innovations in gameplay, narrative, and world design that capitalize on advances in hardware and player expectations.

The dual-protagonist structure suggested by Jason and Lucia hints at a narrative approach that may echo elements from Grand Theft Auto V, which featured three playable characters. Details about the game's setting, mechanics, and story remain largely under wraps, though the character descriptions suggest a storyline centered on individuals facing systemic disadvantage—a thematic thread consistent with the series' satirical approach to crime narratives.

The November 19 launch date positions GTA VI as a major holiday release, coming ahead of the year-end gaming season. For Take-Two Interactive and Rockstar Games, the title represents a significant opportunity to recapture player attention and maintain the Grand Theft Auto franchise's dominance in the open-world action-adventure market after more than a decade away from the series' core audience.
