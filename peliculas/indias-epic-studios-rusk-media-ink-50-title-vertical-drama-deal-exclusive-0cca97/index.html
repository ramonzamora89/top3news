India’s Epic Studios, Rusk Media Ink 50-Title Vertical Drama Deal (EXCLUSIVE)

What's happening

Epic Studios, a major Indian production company, has signed a landmark deal with Rusk Media Private Limited to create 50 original vertical drama series. The partnership aims to establish India's largest owned library of vertical-drama intellectual property across multiple genres.

Who's involved

Epic Studios is a prominent Indian entertainment production house, while Rusk Media Private Limited (RMPL) is the collaboration partner. Together, they are pooling resources to develop original content specifically formatted for vertical viewing platforms.

Why it matters

This deal signals a major industry shift toward vertical storytelling in India, a format optimized for mobile and short-form streaming consumption. The creation of 50 owned titles positions both companies to capitalize on growing demand for Indian original content and establishes a competitive foothold in the expanding digital entertainment landscape.

The story

India's Epic Studios and Rusk Media Private Limited have announced an exclusive partnership to produce 50 original vertical drama series, marking a significant expansion of homegrown content designed for mobile-first audiences. The ambitious slate will span diverse genres including mythology, drama, and mystery, establishing what the companies describe as the country's largest library of owned vertical-drama intellectual property.

The vertical format represents a deliberate strategic choice in India's rapidly evolving digital entertainment ecosystem. Unlike traditional horizontal cinematography designed for television and cinema screens, vertical drama is optimized for smartphones and tablets, formats that dominate media consumption among India's growing online audience. By committing to 50 titles, Epic Studios and Rusk Media are signaling confidence that this format will continue to drive engagement and viewership.

According to Variety, the partnership reflects broader industry recognition that Indian audiences are consuming entertainment increasingly through digital platforms and mobile devices. The variety of genres planned—from mythological stories rooted in Indian cultural traditions to contemporary drama and mystery narratives—suggests the companies are positioning their library to appeal to multiple audience segments and viewing preferences.

Ownership of intellectual property has become central to streaming strategy in recent years. By retaining full ownership of these 50 titles, Epic Studios and Rusk Media avoid the licensing constraints that often limit content creators' long-term revenue potential. This approach allows the companies to exploit their content across multiple platforms, formats, and distribution channels while maintaining creative and commercial control.

The deal also underscores the growing maturity of India's digital content production infrastructure. A decade ago, vertical video was largely confined to social media platforms and experimental projects. Today, dedicated platforms and audiences have created genuine demand for professionally produced vertical drama series, enabling production companies to build sustainable business models around the format.

As India's streaming market continues to expand and compete globally, partnerships like this one demonstrate how domestic producers are developing distinctive content strategies tailored to local viewing habits rather than simply adapting Western models. The focus on owned intellectual property positions Epic Studios and Rusk Media to benefit from multiple revenue streams and to build enduring entertainment franchises rooted in Indian storytelling traditions.
