Isabelle Huppert, Maggie Gyllenhaal, Kore-Eda Hirokazu, Fan Bingbing, Michelle Yeoh on Guest List as Busan Unveils Galas, Asian Cinema Competition

What's happening

The 31st Busan International Film Festival has announced a prestigious guest lineup featuring acclaimed actors and filmmakers including Isabelle Huppert, Maggie Gyllenhaal, and Michelle Yeoh. The festival is unveiling its gala selections and Asian Cinema Competition programming ahead of the event.

Who's involved

The guest list spans global cinema: French-Polish actor Isabelle Huppert, American actor Maggie Gyllenhaal, Japanese director Kore-eda Hirokazu, Chinese actor Fan Bingbing, and Malaysian-American actor Michelle Yeoh. The Busan International Film Festival, held annually in South Korea's Haeundae District, is one of Asia's most significant film festivals, having launched in 1996 as Korea's first international film festival.

Why it matters

The announcement signals the festival's continued prominence as a major platform for Asian and international cinema, attracting world-renowned talent and high-profile film submissions. The caliber of attendees underscores the festival's role in championing diverse filmmaking and providing a prestigious venue for films competing in its Asian Cinema Competition.

The story

The 31st Busan International Film Festival has pulled together one of its most star-studded guest rosters yet, according to Variety, with Isabelle Huppert, Maggie Gyllenhaal, Kore-eda Hirokazu, Fan Bingbing, and Michelle Yeoh headlining the festival's programming. Huppert is bringing her latest film, "All About Corinne," to screen for Busan audiences, while Gyllenhaal is making her first appearance at the festival.

The Busan International Film Festival, formerly known as the Pusan International Film Festival, stands as one of the most significant film festivals in Asia. Held annually in the Haeundae District of Busan, South Korea, the festival has maintained its prominence since its inaugural edition from September 13 to 21, 1996—marking the first international film festival ever held in Korea. Since that groundbreaking debut nearly three decades ago, BIFF has evolved into a major venue for showcasing both established and emerging filmmakers from across the region and beyond.

The festival's announcement of its gala selections and Asian Cinema Competition programming reflects its dual mission: celebrating accomplished international filmmakers while providing a dedicated platform for Asian cinema. The presence of directors like Kore-eda Hirokazu, known for his distinctive filmmaking voice, alongside established actors from multiple continents, demonstrates the festival's broad reach and appeal within the global film community.

Fan Bingbing and Michelle Yeoh represent particular significance for the festival's Asian Cinema focus, bringing visibility to the region's prolific film industries. The inclusion of such recognized names in the guest lineup typically signals the caliber of films being programmed and the festival's commitment to drawing international attention to its selections.
