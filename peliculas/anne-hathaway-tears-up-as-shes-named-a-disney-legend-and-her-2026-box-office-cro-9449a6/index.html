Anne Hathaway Tears Up as She’s Named a Disney Legend (and Her 2026 Box Office Crosses $2 Billion): ‘I’m One of the Most Insanely Lucky People in the World’

What's happening

Anne Hathaway has been inducted as a Disney Legend during D23, the Walt Disney Company's biennial fan convention. The Oscar-winning actress became emotional upon receiving the honor, marking a major career milestone.

Who's involved

Anne Hathaway is an Academy Award-winning American actress whose films have grossed over $6.8 billion worldwide. The Walt Disney Company, through its D23 fan convention, formally recognizes outstanding contributors to Disney's entertainment legacy.

Why it matters

The Disney Legend designation represents formal recognition of Hathaway's significant contributions to Disney's film catalog and cultural impact. The honor underscores her status as one of Hollywood's most commercially successful and critically acclaimed performers, with her box office totals continuing to grow.

The story

Anne Hathaway officially joined Disney's most elite circle of entertainment legends on Sunday afternoon when she was inducted into the company's hall of fame at D23, the Walt Disney Company's biennial convention celebrating its fan community and corporate milestones. The emotional moment saw Hathaway acknowledging the magnitude of her career trajectory, expressing gratitude for the opportunities that have defined her professional life.

Hathaway's selection as a Disney Legend reflects decades of collaborations with the studio that have produced some of her most iconic and commercially successful roles. An Academy Award winner whose accolades also include a British Academy Film Award, a Golden Globe Award, and a Primetime Emmy Award, Hathaway has become one of cinema's most bankable stars. Her films have collectively earned over $6.8 billion worldwide, cementing her status as a major commercial and critical force in entertainment.

According to Variety, Hathaway's reaction to the honor was visibly emotional, with the actress expressing how deeply the recognition resonated with her. She described herself as "one of the most insanely lucky people in the world," a sentiment that reflected both her humility and genuine appreciation for her career's trajectory. The timing of the announcement coincided with news that her box office earnings had surpassed $2 billion, a milestone that further underscored her unparalleled success as a leading actress in major studio productions.

The Disney Legend designation places Hathaway among an exclusive group of entertainers whose contributions have fundamentally shaped the company's brand and cultural relevance. The honor is typically reserved for those who have demonstrated exceptional talent, longevity, and impact within the Disney ecosystem. For Hathaway, the recognition validates her evolution from emerging talent to one of Hollywood's most consistently bankable and artistically respected performers.

The D23 convention, where the induction took place, serves as the official gathering space for Disney's most devoted fans and industry stakeholders. The biennial event features announcements, celebrations, and tributes that define the company's relationship with its audience and its own legacy. Hathaway's induction as a Disney Legend adds to the ceremony's significance, marking a formal acknowledgment of her irreplaceable role in the studio's recent decades of filmmaking.
