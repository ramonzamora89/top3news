Unscripted Producer Rachel Tung Renews ABC & Hulu Development Deal (EXCLUSIVE)

What's happening

Veteran unscripted producer Rachel Tung has renewed her development deal with ABC and Hulu, securing continued opportunities to create original programming for Disney's broadcast and streaming platforms. Under the refreshed agreement, Tung will develop new projects while serving as showrunner on select series.

Who's involved

Rachel Tung is an established unscripted television producer with a track record of developing reality and documentary-style content. ABC and Hulu, both part of The Walt Disney Company, are the broadcast and streaming partners backing her production efforts.

Why it matters

The renewal signals Disney's confidence in Tung's ability to generate compelling unscripted content across multiple platforms at a time when networks are heavily investing in reality programming. Long-term, this deal positions Tung to shape the unscripted landscape on both traditional broadcast and streaming services, where demand for diverse, original programming continues to grow.

The story

Veteran unscripted producer Rachel Tung has secured a renewed development deal with ABC and Hulu, according to Variety, reflecting ongoing confidence in her ability to create original programming for Disney's portfolio. The extended agreement allows Tung to continue developing unscripted projects while taking on showrunner responsibilities for select series across the network and streaming service.

Tung's career in unscripted television has established her as a capable producer capable of navigating both the demands of broadcast television and the distinct requirements of streaming platforms. The renewal comes at a moment when traditional networks and streamers are competing intensely for original content that can differentiate their offerings and retain audiences.

Under the terms of the renewed deal, Tung will focus on developing original unscripted programming tailored to ABC and Hulu's distinct audiences and programming strategies. Her role as showrunner on select projects indicates that the partnership values not only her creative development capabilities but also her ability to oversee production and maintain creative vision through execution.

The agreement underscores Disney's commitment to maintaining relationships with established producers who have demonstrated success in the unscripted space. As streaming services and traditional networks continue to blur in their content strategies, producers like Tung who can work effectively across both formats are increasingly valuable to major media companies.

Tung's renewed deal comes amid broader industry trends of networks retaining proven creative talent through multi-year agreements. Such arrangements provide stability for producers while allowing studios to maintain pipelines of original content development.
