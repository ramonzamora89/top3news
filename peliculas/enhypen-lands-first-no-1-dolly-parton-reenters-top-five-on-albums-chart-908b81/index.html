Enhypen Lands First No. 1, Dolly Parton Reenters Top Five on Albums Chart

What's happening

Enhypen, a South Korean boy band, has claimed its first No. 1 album on the Billboard 200 with "The Sin: Bliss," moving 98,000 equivalent units in its debut week. Dolly Parton's catalog album "Ultimate Dolly Parton" has simultaneously reentered the top five following the country music icon's death earlier this week.

Who's involved

Enhypen is a six-member South Korean boy band formed by Belift Lab through the 2020 survival competition show I-Land, debuting in November 2020. Dolly Parton was a legendary American country music singer-songwriter, actress, and businesswoman known as the "Queen of Country," with over 100 million records sold and a legacy spanning philanthropy and cultural influence.

Why it matters

Enhypen's first chart-topper marks a significant milestone for the group and reflects the continued global dominance of K-pop in mainstream music markets. Dolly Parton's chart resurgence demonstrates the enduring commercial power of her catalog and the emotional resonance of her passing, while illustrating how artist deaths often trigger renewed fan engagement with their work.

The story

Enhypen has achieved a major career milestone by landing its first No. 1 album on the Billboard 200 chart with "The Sin: Bliss." According to Variety, the South Korean boy band moved 98,000 equivalent album units during its first week of release, securing the coveted top spot.

Enhypen, formed through the 2020 survival competition show I-Land, has grown into one of K-pop's most prominent acts since their November 2020 debut. The six-member group, consisting of Jay, Jake, Sunghoon, Sunoo, Jungwon, and Ni-Ki, initially debuted as a seven-piece ensemble before changes to their roster. Their breakthrough onto the Billboard 200's summit represents a significant achievement for the Belift Lab-managed group and underscores their expanding influence in the American music market.

Simultaneously, the chart saw an emotional resurgence as Dolly Parton's "Ultimate Dolly Parton" reentered the top five this week, following news of the country music legend's death. Parton, who earned her nickname "Queen of Country" through decades of groundbreaking recordings and performances, left an indelible mark on American popular culture and the music industry at large.

Parton's commercial legacy remains formidable: with more than 100 million records sold throughout her lifetime, she ranks among the best-selling music artists of all time. Beyond her recording achievements, she became known for her cultural influence and substantial philanthropic work, including the establishment of Dollywood and the Dollywood Foundation, which have touched countless lives through entertainment, education, and community support.

The simultaneous presence of Enhypen and Parton on the charts this week illustrates the complex dynamics of the modern music industry, where new artist breakthroughs and catalog reactivations frequently occur in parallel. For Enhypen, the No. 1 ranking validates their artistic evolution and their capacity to resonate with American audiences on the Billboard 200, historically one of the most competitive charts in the music business. For Parton's estate and fans, the renewed chart presence serves as a testament to the timeless quality of her music and its continued relevance across generations.
