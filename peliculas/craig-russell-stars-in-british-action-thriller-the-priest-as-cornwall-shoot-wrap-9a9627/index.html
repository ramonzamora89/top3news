Craig Russell Stars in British Action Thriller ‘The Priest’ as Cornwall Shoot Wraps (EXCLUSIVE)

What's happening

Craig Russell has completed filming on "The Priest," a British action thriller in which he plays a former operative masquerading as a clergy member. The production wrapped its location shoot in Cornwall, marking the feature directorial debut of Will Saunders.

Who's involved

Craig Russell, known for roles in "Protein" and "Queen Cleopatra," leads the cast. Will Saunders directs his first feature film with this project, which was shot on location in Cornwall, U.K.

Why it matters

The project represents a significant milestone for debut filmmaker Saunders and showcases Russell in a lead role within the action-thriller genre. As the film moves from production into post-production, it positions itself within the landscape of British genre cinema, potentially offering audiences a homegrown action vehicle.

The story

Craig Russell has wrapped filming on "The Priest," a British action thriller that showcases the actor in a lead role as a former operative concealing his past behind the collar of a priest. The production completed its location shoot in Cornwall, U.K., according to Variety, marking a significant moment for the film's development as it transitions into post-production phases.

The film represents the feature directorial debut of Will Saunders, an opportunity that brings a new voice to British cinema's action-thriller space. Russell, whose recent television credits include "Protein" and "Queen Cleopatra," steps into the central role of a character navigating the tension between his covert past and his present spiritual cover. The actor is represented by agents working in the industry to bring the project to completion and eventual distribution.

Cornwall's dramatic coastlines and varied locations provided the backdrop for the shoot, a choice common among British productions seeking both distinctive visual geography and production efficiency. The region has increasingly become a destination for film and television work, offering crews access to varied landscapes within a concentrated area.

With principal photography concluded, the filmmaking team will now focus on editing, visual effects, sound design, and color grading—the technical dimensions that transform raw footage into a finished feature. The thriller genre's demands for precise pacing and impactful sequences make post-production particularly crucial for action-heavy narratives. How the creative team shapes the material during these stages will significantly influence the film's eventual reception when it enters the marketplace for distribution and exhibition.

The completion of the shoot represents progress for a project that has moved from development and pre-production into tangible finished material. As the industry continues to produce genre content, debuts from new directorial voices like Saunders contribute to the ongoing diversity of British filmmaking.
