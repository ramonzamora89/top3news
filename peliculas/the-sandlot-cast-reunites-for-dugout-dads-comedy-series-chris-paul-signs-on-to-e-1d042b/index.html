‘The Sandlot’ Cast Reunites for ‘Dugout Dads’ Comedy Series, Chris Paul Signs on to Executive Produce

What's happening

The cast of the 1993 film 'The Sandlot' is reuniting for a new comedy series called 'Dugout Dads,' a nine-episode short-form show streaming on YouTube. NBA star Chris Paul has signed on as an executive producer for the project.

Who's involved

Patrick Renna and Kyle Howard, creators behind Hambino Media, are developing the series and bringing together the original 'Sandlot' ensemble cast. Chris Paul, a former NBA point guard widely regarded as one of the greatest at his position, is joining as an executive producer to help expand the show's reach and profile.

Why it matters

The collaboration represents a high-profile validation of the streaming comedy project, potentially helping it secure wider distribution and investment. Paul's involvement signals growing crossover between professional sports figures and entertainment production, while the reunion taps into nostalgia for the beloved 1993 sports film to attract both original fans and new audiences.

The story

The cast of 'The Sandlot,' the 1993 coming-of-age sports comedy that launched the careers of several young actors, is reuniting for a new project titled 'Dugout Dads.' The nine-episode comedy series, created by Patrick Renna and Kyle Howard through their production company Hambino Media, is currently streaming on YouTube but is actively seeking expanded distribution and a larger platform.

'The Sandlot,' which was written and directed by David Mickey Evans, originally told the story of a diverse group of young baseball players during the summer of 1962 in the San Fernando Valley. The 1993 film became a cultural touchstone for multiple generations of viewers and established itself as a beloved sports comedy classic.

The new 'Dugout Dads' series continues in the same spirit of sports-centered storytelling, offering another coming-of-age narrative centered around baseball. By reuniting the original cast members decades later, the project aims to blend nostalgia with fresh creative material designed to appeal to both longtime fans and audiences discovering the franchise for the first time.

In a significant endorsement of the project, NBA legend Chris Paul has joined the series as an executive producer. Paul, nicknamed "CP3" and "the Point God," is regarded as one of the greatest point guards in basketball history. His accolades include NBA Rookie of the Year, an NBA All-Star Game Most Valuable Player Award, two Olympic gold medals, five league-leading assists titles, and a record six steals titles. His involvement brings both star power and industry credibility to the YouTube series, according to Variety.

The addition of Paul to the creative and business team underscores a growing trend of elite athletes diversifying into entertainment production. His executive producer credit suggests the series is positioning itself for growth beyond its current YouTube platform, with industry figures like Paul potentially opening doors to streaming partnerships or traditional media distribution.

With original cast members returning to the fold and high-profile backing in place, 'Dugout Dads' represents an attempt to capitalize on the enduring appeal of baseball-themed storytelling and the particular affection audiences hold for 'The Sandlot' legacy.
