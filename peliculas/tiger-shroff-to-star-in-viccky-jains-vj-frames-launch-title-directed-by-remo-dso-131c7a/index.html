Tiger Shroff to Star in Viccky Jain’s VJ Frames Launch Title, Directed by Remo D’Souza, With Abhishek Banerjee, Elvish Yadav Also Set (EXCLUSIVE)

What's happening

Producer Viccky Jain has launched a new production company called VJ Frames, with its debut project being an action franchise directed by choreographer-turned-filmmaker Remo D'Souza and starring Tiger Shroff, Abhishek Banerjee, and Elvish Yadav.

Who's involved

Viccky Jain is an Indian producer and entrepreneur launching his new banner VJ Frames. The project brings together director Remo D'Souza, a renowned choreographer and filmmaker with over 25 years in the industry, and actor Tiger Shroff, who made his debut in Heropanti (2014) and has established himself as a leading action hero in Hindi cinema.

Why it matters

The launch signals fresh momentum in Hindi cinema's action-franchise space, with an established director and bankable star teaming on a new producer's flagship project. For Jain, VJ Frames represents a major entrepreneurial milestone, while the venture could expand opportunities for emerging talent like Elvish Yadav in mainstream feature films.

The story

Producer and entrepreneur Viccky Jain has officially launched VJ Frames, a new production banner timed to his birthday, according to Variety. The company's debut project is an action franchise helmed by Remo D'Souza, the celebrated choreographer and filmmaker, with a cast anchored by Tiger Shroff alongside actors Abhishek Banerjee and content creator Elvish Yadav.

Remo D'Souza brings formidable credentials to the project. Over a career spanning more than 25 years, he has choreographed more than 100 films and is widely regarded as one of the most successful choreographers in the Hindi film industry. Beyond his work as a dancer and choreographer, D'Souza has transitioned into directing and producing, establishing himself as a multifaceted creative force capable of handling large-scale action narratives.

Tiger Shroff, the lead actor, has built a distinctive brand around high-octane action sequences since his acting debut in the 2014 action romance Heropanti, for which he won the IIFA Award for Star Debut of the Year – Male. His combination of dance training and physical prowess has made him a natural fit for franchise-driven projects, and his casting signals VJ Frames' commitment to delivering spectacle-driven entertainment.

The inclusion of Abhishek Banerjee and Elvish Yadav rounds out an ensemble that blends established Hindi film talent with digital-age celebrity. Elvish Yadav's presence is particularly notable, as it represents a broader trend of digital creators crossing into theatrical releases, potentially widening the film's appeal across demographic segments.

VJ Frames' launch as a production house marks a significant expansion for Jain in the entertainment sector. The timing of the announcement with his birthday adds a personal dimension to what is fundamentally a major business move, positioning the new banner to establish its identity through an ambitious action franchise as its opening statement to the industry.
