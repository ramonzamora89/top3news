Apple’s ‘new’ polishing cloth is the same except $10 cheaper

What's happening

Apple has released a new polishing cloth priced at $9, down from the original $19 version. The product maintains the same soft, nonabrasive material specifications but undercuts its predecessor by 50 percent.

Who's involved

Apple Inc., the American multinational technology company headquartered in Cupertino, California, is releasing the discounted cloth. MacRumors, a website that aggregates Apple-related news and rumors, first spotted the price reduction.

Why it matters

The price cut signals a potential shift in Apple's premium pricing strategy for accessories, suggesting the company may be recalibrating its polishing cloth as a value item rather than a luxury accessory. This move comes as Apple continues refreshing its product lineup, including new Mac Mini models, offering consumers cost savings on complementary products.

The story

Apple has quietly introduced a new version of its polishing cloth at a significantly reduced price point. The cloth, which now costs $9, represents a 50 percent discount from the original $19 price tag that accompanied Apple's initial launch of the premium accessory. According to MacRumors, the specifications remain largely unchanged—the cloth is still manufactured from soft, nonabrasive material designed for cleaning Apple's displays and other sensitive surfaces.

The timing of this release is noteworthy given Apple's broader product refresh cycle. The company has just unveiled refreshed Mac Mini models alongside the polishing cloth release, yet unlike those computers, the cloth arrives with consumer-friendly pricing rather than premium positioning. This contrasts with Apple's typical approach to accessories, which have often commanded high prices relative to functionally similar third-party alternatives.

Apple Inc., founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs, Steve Wozniak, and Ronald Wayne, has built its reputation on premium consumer electronics and carefully curated product ecosystems. The company's current focus spans consumer electronics, software, and online services following its expansion beyond computers beginning in 2007. Historically, Apple's accessories have reflected this premium positioning, with products priced to reinforce the brand's luxury market perception.

The polishing cloth itself emerged as an unlikely focal point of discussion when Apple first introduced it alongside the high-end Studio Display and other premium products. At $19, the accessory became a subject of industry commentary, with observers noting the price seemed steep for a simple cleaning cloth. The new $9 version suggests Apple may have reconsidered this positioning, opting instead to make the product more accessible to a broader audience.

Whether this price adjustment indicates a permanent shift in Apple's accessory strategy remains unclear. The reduction could reflect customer feedback, competitive pressure, or simply a decision to position the cloth as an entry-level accessory rather than a premium product. Regardless, the move offers existing and prospective customers a more economical way to maintain their Apple devices with manufacturer-approved materials.
