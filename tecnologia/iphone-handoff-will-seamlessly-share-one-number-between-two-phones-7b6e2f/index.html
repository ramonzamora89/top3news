iPhone Handoff will seamlessly share one number between two phones

What's happening

Apple is rolling out a new feature called iPhone Handoff with iOS 27 later this month that allows users to seamlessly switch between two phones while maintaining the same phone number. The capability was teased during Apple's WWDC keynote in June, but now demo footage reveals how the feature actually works.

Who's involved

Apple Inc., the Cupertino-based technology company, is introducing this feature as part of its iOS 27 update. The feature was first unveiled at WWDC, Apple's annual Worldwide Developers Conference held at Apple Park, where the company showcases new software and technologies across its device ecosystem.

Why it matters

iPhone Handoff addresses a long-standing inconvenience for users who own multiple iPhones—the inability to use a single phone number across devices simultaneously. This capability could appeal to professionals who switch between work and personal phones, or users who upgrade devices, reducing the friction of managing separate numbers and improving the continuity of Apple's ecosystem.

The story

Apple is preparing to launch iPhone Handoff, a feature designed to let users operate two phones with a single phone number, arriving with iOS 27 later this month. The functionality was first mentioned during Apple's WWDC keynote in June, though specifics were sparse at that time. Now, according to The Verge, demo footage has emerged showing exactly how the feature will operate.

The new capability addresses a practical pain point for iPhone users who own multiple devices. Traditionally, maintaining separate phone numbers across different iPhones has been a limitation, forcing users to choose which device receives calls and messages or resort to forwarding arrangements. iPhone Handoff aims to eliminate that friction by enabling true number sharing between two phones, allowing seamless transitions between devices without the user having to manage multiple phone lines or complex call-forwarding setups.

Apple Inc., the multinational technology company headquartered in Silicon Valley, has long emphasized ecosystem continuity as a central feature of its product line. Features like this one build on that philosophy by deepening integration across multiple devices. The Worldwide Developers Conference, held annually at Apple Park in Cupertino since 2020, serves as the company's primary venue for unveiling such software innovations to both the developer community and the public. The initial mention of iPhone Handoff at WWDC in June signaled the company's commitment to addressing device connectivity challenges.

The demo material now available provides concrete details about how the feature functions in practice, though the full technical requirements and any limitations remain to be seen when iOS 27 officially launches. Users have been anticipating clearer information since the feature's initial announcement, and the visual demonstration offers the first substantive look at the capability in action. The feature represents another step in Apple's ongoing effort to make its ecosystem more interconnected and convenient for users managing multiple devices.
