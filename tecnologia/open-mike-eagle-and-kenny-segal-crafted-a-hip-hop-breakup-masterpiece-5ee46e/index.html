Open Mike Eagle and Kenny Segal crafted a hip hop breakup masterpiece

What's happening

Open Mike Eagle and producer Kenny Segal have released DOOMED!, a hip-hop album centered on the theme of breakups. The project uses a breakup narrative as its overarching concept, with an interlude titled "It Happens in Every Universe" serving as the record's emotional thesis.

Who's involved

Open Mike Eagle is a Chicago-born rapper, producer, and comedian now based in Los Angeles, where he is a member of the hip-hop collective Project Blowed. Kenny Segal, also known as Syndakit, is a Los Angeles-based producer and DJ whom Mixmag described in 2018 as "one of the best hip-hop producers in the city."

Why it matters

The collaboration represents a significant creative partnership between two respected figures in the underground hip-hop scene, combining Eagle's lyricism and comedic sensibility with Segal's production expertise. Albums that explore emotional vulnerability and specific life experiences like breakups can resonate deeply with audiences and contribute to hip-hop's continued evolution as a vehicle for personal storytelling.

The story

Open Mike Eagle and Kenny Segal have crafted what The Verge describes as a hip-hop breakup masterpiece with their album DOOMED!. The record opens with a deceptively simple observation—"Breakups are… tough"—delivered in an interlude positioned toward the project's end, titled "It Happens in Every Universe." This brief statement serves as the emotional and thematic anchor for the entire album, suggesting that heartbreak transcends circumstance and is a universal human experience.

Eagle, a Chicago native who relocated to Los Angeles and became integral to the Project Blowed collective, brings his distinctive voice to the project. His background as a rapper, producer, and comedian gives him multiple dimensions through which to explore the subject matter. Collaborating with him is Segal, a Los Angeles-based producer whose production work has earned recognition in the industry; Mixmag identified him as one of the city's best hip-hop producers in 2018. The partnership merges Eagle's introspective lyricism with Segal's refined sonic palette.

The album's conceptual approach to breakups distinguishes it within the hip-hop landscape. Rather than scattered references to heartbreak, DOOMED! treats the experience as a through-line, using the album's structure to guide listeners through the emotional journey. The placement of the thesis statement near the album's conclusion suggests a cyclical understanding of breakups—that they repeat across time and circumstance, making the closing philosophy resonate more powerfully after the listener has traveled through the preceding material.

The interlude's title, "It Happens in Every Universe," adds a layer of philosophical speculation to what could have been a straightforward breakup album. By invoking the multiverse concept, Eagle and Segal suggest that certain experiences—particularly romantic loss—are so fundamental to human existence that they occur regardless of circumstance or timeline. This approach elevates the album beyond mere catharsis into something more conceptually ambitious.

The project stands as evidence of the continued vitality of concept-driven hip-hop and the willingness of artists to explore vulnerability in the genre. By centering an entire album around a single emotional event and its universal resonance, Eagle and Segal create a focused artistic statement that avoids the trap of scattered subject matter.
