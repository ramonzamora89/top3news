Samsung has new Galaxy headphones in the works

What's happening

Samsung is developing a new pair of over-ear headphones called the Galaxy H1, according to code discovered in its Galaxy Wearable app. The device is positioned as a competitor to Apple's premium AirPods Max.

Who's involved

Samsung, the South Korean multinational conglomerate and world's fifth-highest valued brand, is building the headphones. Apple's AirPods Max, released in December 2020 as the company's highest-end audio option, serve as the market reference point.

Why it matters

This signals Samsung's push deeper into premium audio hardware, a category dominated by Apple in the high-end segment. If launched, the Galaxy H1 could reshape competitive dynamics in the over-ear wireless headphones market and give Samsung users a flagship audio accessory tightly integrated with their ecosystem.

The story

Samsung appears to be preparing a challenge to Apple's premium headphone offerings. Code found within Samsung's Galaxy Wearable app references an upcoming device called the Galaxy H1, suggesting the company is developing over-ear headphones designed to compete with Apple's AirPods Max. SamMobile reports that the discovery hints at a potential launch sometime in the near future, though Samsung has not yet made any official announcements.

The move represents Samsung's expansion into higher-end audio hardware. While Samsung has offered various earbuds and audio accessories under the Galaxy brand, purpose-built over-ear headphones at a premium tier would mark a more aggressive positioning in the segment currently dominated by Apple. AirPods Max, which launched in December 2020, are Apple's highest-end audio product, sitting at the top of a lineup that includes the base AirPods and the mid-range AirPods Pro.

Samsung's timing and strategy suggest an attempt to capture consumers who own Samsung devices and may currently purchase Apple's premium headphones due to ecosystem integration and lack of comparable alternatives. By developing hardware explicitly named for its Galaxy product family, Samsung is signaling that the H1 is intended as a cornerstone device for users already committed to its smartphone and wearables ecosystem.

The discovery of the Galaxy H1 in app code is a common harbinger of upcoming consumer electronics launches, allowing researchers and tech outlets to identify product plans before manufacturers are ready for public disclosure. The presence of references in the Galaxy Wearable app—software that manages Samsung's connected devices—suggests the headphones are designed to work seamlessly with Samsung phones, tablets, and watches.

Without official specifications or a confirmed launch window, details remain sparse. The internal naming suggests Samsung may eventually market the headphones under a simpler commercial name, though the H1 designation could carry through to the final product. For now, the discovery indicates that Samsung is actively investing in premium audio hardware as part of its broader strategy to compete across consumer electronics categories where Apple has established strong market positions.
