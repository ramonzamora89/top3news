With Switch 2, iPhone, and laptop tricks, the Sharge Disk Pro 2 is finally a worthy EDC

What's happening

Sharge has released the Disk Pro 2, a portable SSD that finally delivers on the company's promise of quality storage hardware. The device combines fast performance with practical features for everyday carry, marking a significant improvement over previous Sharge storage products.

Who's involved

Sharge is a tech accessory company known for producing innovative chargers styled after vintage Mac designs and transparent batteries with visible internal components. The Verge's Sean Hollister is reviewing the product, a publication known for rigorous hardware evaluation.

Why it matters

This release signals that Sharge is maturing beyond novelty accessories into genuinely functional premium hardware, potentially expanding its market reach beyond design-conscious early adopters. A reliable portable SSD from Sharge could establish the company as a serious competitor in the crowded external storage market.

More context

Sharge previously released the original Disk, Disk Plus, and Disk Pro models, none of which earned strong recommendations from reviewers. The company has built brand recognition through retro-styled chargers and transparent batteries that appeal to users seeking distinctive design alongside functionality. The Disk Pro 2 apparently addresses previous shortcomings with improved features and performance, suggesting the company listened to feedback. Portable SSDs remain popular for content creators and professionals requiring fast, reliable external storage for daily use.
