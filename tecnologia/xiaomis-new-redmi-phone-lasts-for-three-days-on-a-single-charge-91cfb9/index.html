Xiaomi’s new Redmi phone lasts for three days on a single charge

What's happening

Xiaomi's Redmi subsidiary has unveiled the Note 17 Pro Max, featuring a massive 9,210mAh dual-cell battery that enables three days of usage on a single charge. The phone also incorporates a photochromic design that changes appearance when exposed to sunlight.

Who's involved

Xiaomi is a Chinese multinational technology company and the world's third-largest smartphone seller as of 2025. Redmi, launched as a separate sub-brand in 2019, handles Xiaomi's entry-level and mid-range devices, including this new flagship battery offering.

Why it matters

The advancement represents a significant milestone in smartphone battery technology, directly addressing one of consumers' most persistent complaints about mobile devices. With 754.1 million global monthly active users, Xiaomi's battery innovation could influence industry standards and set expectations for competitors across the sector.

The story

Xiaomi's Redmi brand is pushing the boundaries of smartphone battery capacity with the announcement of the Note 17 Pro Max, a device that achieves an impressive three days of battery life on a single charge. The accomplishment hinges on the integration of a 9,210mAh dual-cell battery, the largest capacity Xiaomi has ever installed in any phone sold in Europe, according to The Verge, with even larger configurations reportedly available in certain other markets.

The Note 17 Pro Max distinguishes itself not only through its battery prowess but also through an innovative photochromic design feature. This design element remains hidden under normal lighting conditions but reveals itself when exposed to sunlight, creating a dynamic aesthetic that changes based on environmental conditions. The combination of extended battery life and responsive design elements signals Xiaomi's focus on both practical functionality and consumer appeal.

Xiaomi, headquartered in Beijing, has established itself as a major force in global consumer electronics. As the third-largest smartphone seller in the world as of 2025, the company operates through multiple brand tiers. Redmi was established as a separate sub-brand in 2019, specifically to oversee entry-level and mid-range devices while the parent Xiaomi brand focuses on upper-range and flagship products. This strategic division allows Xiaomi to address diverse market segments effectively and maintain strong positioning across price points.

The Redmi Note 17 Pro Max's battery achievement addresses one of the most consistent pain points in smartphone consumer satisfaction: device endurance. Most modern flagship phones require daily charging, making three-day battery life a significant competitive advantage. The dual-cell architecture suggests engineering sophistication designed to manage the thermal and longevity challenges associated with larger battery systems.

With 754.1 million global monthly active users as of December 2025, Xiaomi commands substantial influence over smartphone industry trends and consumer expectations. Advances in battery technology from such a major player often establish benchmarks that competitors must address in subsequent product releases. The Note 17 Pro Max demonstrates that meaningful improvements in practical usability remain possible even as the smartphone market has matured.

The European release of the Note 17 Pro Max marks another step in Redmi's effort to establish itself as a credible alternative to premium-priced competitors. By delivering extended battery capacity at accessible price points, the brand appeals to practical consumers who prioritize longevity and value over cutting-edge specifications alone.
