Lucid Reveals New Cosmos Images, Says Sportier Gravity Coming Soon

What's happening

Lucid Motors has unveiled new images of its upcoming Cosmos model, revealing a sleek design with a sloping roofline. The company is also promising a high-performance variant of its Gravity SUV sooner than initially expected.

Who's involved

Lucid Motors is an American EV manufacturer headquartered in Newark, California, which began producing the Lucid Air sedan in 2021 and started Gravity SUV production in December 2024. The company is now expanding its lineup with new models and performance variants.

Why it matters

The Cosmos and sportier Gravity represent Lucid's strategy to broaden its product range beyond the Air sedan and capture different market segments in the growing luxury EV space. Accelerating the high-performance Gravity's arrival could help Lucid compete more effectively against established luxury automakers while ramping up overall production volumes.

The story

Lucid Motors is moving forward with its expansion plans, revealing new imagery of the upcoming Cosmos model while signaling that a more powerful version of its Gravity SUV will arrive ahead of schedule. According to Car and Driver, the Cosmos features a distinctive sloping roofline and could begin production as soon as next year, positioning it as another key addition to Lucid's growing electric vehicle lineup.

The Cosmos represents Lucid's third vehicle offering, following the Lucid Air sedan, which the company began manufacturing at its Casa Grande, Arizona factory in September 2021, and the Lucid Gravity SUV, which launched production in December 2024. The mid-size luxury crossover Gravity has already established itself as the brand's first SUV and second vehicle overall, arriving with a multitude of features and amenities aimed at the premium market segment.

The high-performance variant of the Gravity is particularly notable given its accelerated timeline. By bringing the sportier iteration to market sooner than previously announced, Lucid aims to offer customers a more dynamic option within its SUV portfolio while capitalizing on growing consumer interest in performance-oriented electric vehicles. This move suggests the company is confident in its production capabilities and supply chain as it scales manufacturing operations.

Lucid's portfolio expansion comes as the company continues to establish itself as both a direct automaker and a technology supplier. Beyond manufacturing its own vehicles, Lucid supplies advanced electric vehicle powertrain systems to other automakers, including Aston Martin, diversifying its revenue streams and leveraging its engineering expertise across the industry.

The introduction of the Cosmos adds another dimension to Lucid's strategy, targeting customers who may seek a different design language or market positioning than the Air or Gravity. With potential production starting next year, the timeline suggests the company has finalized development and is preparing manufacturing infrastructure to support the new model.

As Lucid navigates an increasingly competitive luxury EV market, the combination of new model launches and accelerated high-performance variants underscores the company's commitment to offering diverse options within its growing product family.
