Ram Rumble Bee-Based Hennessey Maximus Is a 1000-HP Street Truck

What's happening

Hennessey Performance Engineering has teased a heavily modified Ram pickup truck called the Rumble Bee-Based Maximus, equipped with a supercharged Hellcat V-8 engine producing 1000 horsepower. The high-performance truck features an aggressive rear wing and claims a top speed of 200 mph.

Who's involved

Hennessey Performance Engineering is an American automotive tuning company and sports car manufacturer known for building extreme performance vehicles. Ram is a full-size pickup truck brand manufactured by Stellantis North America and marketed since 2010.

Why it matters

This project signals the continued trend of ultra-high-performance street trucks blending luxury with raw power, appealing to enthusiasts willing to pay premium prices for one-off builds. The 1000-hp specification and 200-mph capability push the boundaries of what pickup trucks can achieve in acceleration and top speed, potentially influencing consumer expectations and competitor offerings in the performance truck segment.

The story

Hennessey Performance Engineering has unveiled plans for an extreme pickup truck modification that combines the practicality of a Ram truck with supercar-level performance. The Rumble Bee-Based Hennessey Maximus, according to Car and Driver, features a supercharged Hellcat V-8 engine and a striking rear wing, with the tuner claiming the vehicle can reach 200 miles per hour.

The engine package represents the centerpiece of the build. By leveraging the supercharged Hellcat V-8—a proven powerplant known for delivering exceptional output—Hennessey aims to extract 1000 horsepower from the modified truck. This power figure places the Maximus in rarified territory, as most production trucks max out well below such numbers. The engineering challenge of integrating a supercharged engine of this caliber into a pickup platform designed for hauling and towing demands significant chassis and drivetrain modifications.

The aesthetic modifications signal that this is no ordinary work truck. The prominent rear wing, a component typically found on race cars and high-performance supercars, gives the Maximus a visually aggressive stance. This styling choice underscores Hennessey's philosophy: creating a street truck that performs as dramatically as it looks. Such visual cues serve both functional and psychological purposes, telegraphing the vehicle's performance capability to observers.

Hennessey Performance Engineering has built a reputation as an automotive tuning company willing to tackle ambitious performance projects. Ram, manufactured by Stellantis North America and marketed since 2010, has become a popular donor platform for high-performance builds as the current generation offers modern chassis technology and structural integrity capable of handling significant power increases. The pairing of Hennessey's tuning expertise with Ram's platform appears designed to create a showcase vehicle that demonstrates what is possible when conventional truck architecture meets unlimited performance ambition.

The claimed 200-mph top speed would make the Maximus one of the fastest production or modified trucks ever built. Achieving such speeds requires not only raw horsepower but also aerodynamic refinement, braking capability, and suspension tuning—elements that suggest Hennessey's build goes far beyond a simple engine upgrade. While details remain limited at the teasing stage, the specifications hint at a comprehensive overhaul aimed at creating a functional yet extreme street vehicle.
