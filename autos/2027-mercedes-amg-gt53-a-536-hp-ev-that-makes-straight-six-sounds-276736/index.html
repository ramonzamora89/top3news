2027 Mercedes-AMG GT53: A 536-HP EV That Makes Straight-Six Sounds

What's happening

Mercedes-AMG is introducing a new entry-level GT53 variant for 2027 that runs on electric power and produces 536 horsepower. Despite being significantly less powerful than the 1153-hp GT63 model, the EV will deliver impressive performance while featuring a unique straight-six sound profile.

Who's involved

Mercedes-AMG, the high-performance subsidiary of Mercedes-Benz AG headquartered in Affalterbach, Germany, is developing this new variant. Mercedes-Benz AG, the Stuttgart-based German luxury automaker founded in 1926, manufactures the GT lineup under its AMG performance brand.

Why it matters

The GT53 EV represents Mercedes-AMG's continued shift toward electrification while maintaining performance credentials and brand identity. This entry-level option could expand the high-performance EV market by offering accessible performance to a broader customer base, signaling how traditional luxury performance brands are adapting their powertrains for the electric era.

The story

Mercedes-AMG is preparing to launch a new entry-level variant of its iconic GT sports car, the 2027 GT53, which will mark a significant milestone for the performance brand: a fully electric iteration that delivers 536 horsepower while emulating the auditory signature of a straight-six engine. According to Car and Driver, this new model will be less powerful than the conventional 1153-hp GT63, yet will still offer the acceleration and handling characteristics expected from Mercedes-AMG's elite performance lineup.

The introduction of the GT53 EV underscores Mercedes-AMG's broader electrification strategy. As the high-performance subsidiary of Mercedes-Benz AG—the Stuttgart-based German automaker founded in 1926—AMG has historically focused on optimizing conventional powertrains through engineering innovation and customization. The shift to electric propulsion represents a fundamental pivot in how the brand approaches performance, moving from internal combustion horsepower races to electric motor efficiency and instant torque delivery.

One of the most intriguing aspects of the GT53 is its engineered sound design. Electric vehicles traditionally lack the mechanical noise signatures that enthusiasts associate with performance cars, particularly the distinctive rumble of a multi-cylinder engine. By programming a straight-six sound profile into the EV, Mercedes-AMG is attempting to preserve a crucial element of the driving experience and brand heritage, even as the underlying technology becomes fully electric.

The 536-hp output positions the GT53 as an accessible entry point into the GT family for performance-minded buyers. While substantially outgunned on paper by the GT63, the electric variant's instant torque characteristics and the responsiveness typical of EV powertrains could allow it to feel more agile in real-world driving. This performance democratization aligns with broader trends in the luxury automotive sector, where electrification is expanding rather than contracting the performance segment.

For Mercedes-Benz AG and its AMG division, the GT53 EV also represents a strategic response to competitive pressures in the electric performance market. Other luxury brands have begun populating the high-performance EV space, and offering multiple variants of an iconic nameplate helps AMG maintain its positioning as a premier performance authority while transitioning its portfolio toward electrification.

The 2027 model year timing suggests that development and regulatory certification are well underway, indicating a serious commitment to bringing this vehicle to market within the decade. As the automotive industry continues its acceleration toward electrification, models like the GT53 will increasingly define what performance means for the next generation of luxury car buyers.
