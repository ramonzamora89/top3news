Vote for This Awesome Colin McRae Subaru Rally Lego Set

What's happening

A Lego set design featuring Colin McRae's iconic Subaru rally car is now open for fan voting. If the design receives enough support, it could be produced as an official Lego set.

Who's involved

Lego, the Danish toy company behind the fan-design platform; Colin McRae, the legendary British rally driver who won the 1995 World Rally Championship; and Subaru, whose World Rally Team fielded McRae's distinctive blue-and-yellow cars throughout the 1990s.

Why it matters

This represents a rare intersection of automotive heritage and consumer product design, allowing fans to directly influence which pieces of racing history get immortalized in toy form. A successful campaign could introduce McRae's legacy and Subaru's rally dominance to a new generation of enthusiasts and collectors.

The story

A newly designed Lego set commemorating Colin McRae's legendary Subaru rally car is now available for fan votes on the Lego Ideas platform, according to Car and Driver. If the design garners sufficient community support, it could join Lego's official product lineup as a finished set.

Colin McRae remains one of rally racing's most celebrated figures. The British driver claimed the 1991 and 1992 British Rally Championships before making history in 1995 as the first British driver to win the World Rally Championship Drivers' title. His success came largely during his years with Subaru's World Rally Team, where he piloted the manufacturer's now-iconic blue-and-yellow machines that became synonymous with the sport.

The distinctive color scheme McRae's cars carried—Subaru's signature blue paired with bright yellow—originated from the team's sponsorship deal with State Express 555, a BAT cigarette brand that was particularly popular in Asia. This livery appeared on Subaru vehicles from 1993 to 2003, with particular prominence in the mid-1990s when McRae was at his peak. The combination has since become instantly recognizable to rally fans and remains culturally significant in automotive circles, even decades after tobacco sponsorship was phased out.

The Lego Ideas platform allows designers to submit original creations and pitch them to the broader Lego community. Sets that accumulate sufficient votes are then reviewed for potential production and commercial release. The McRae Subaru design taps into nostalgia for a golden era of rally racing while appealing to both automotive enthusiasts and Lego collectors who value licensed or historically significant builds.

Subaru's World Rally Team itself dates back to 1980, though the program achieved its greatest prominence in the 1990s under British firm Prodrive's management. That decade saw the manufacturer establish itself as a dominant force in world rally competition, with drivers like McRae piloting the distinctive blue machines to numerous victories and championships. For fans of that era, a Lego recreation of McRae's car represents a tangible way to celebrate a transformative period in both the driver's career and Subaru's motorsport legacy.
